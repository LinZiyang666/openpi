What survives four times the episodes

Ten cells re-run at 416 episodes each. The eight top cells all fall, both floor cells rise — textbook selection bias, because those cells were picked because they scored extreme on 104 episodes.

There is no single best configuration

At 416 episodes the top eight collapse into a band narrower than twice the replay noise floor. Report a first tier of balanced mixtures, not a champion cell.

Pinned PickPlace: the search has no signal here

The same 132-cell matrix re-run over the five transport tasks after every object slot was pinned to one exact mesh instance (pin_id 4d13ac5e…), against a 250-episode teacher floor on the identical scene and seed segment.

Best cell sits below the noise ceiling

  • Observed spread across 132 cells: mean 0.0458, sd 0.0344, max 0.1250.
  • Expected maximum of 132 cells under the pure-noise null: 0.1502 (p95 0.2000).
  • Winner's curse for this arm: 10.43 pp; single-cell noise floor 3.44 pp.

The observed best is below what pure sampling noise produces. No weight configuration in this arm is demonstrably better than any other, so no "best weights" may be reported for the pinned pick family.

What this means for the warm-start run

Freeze one config per executor

  • GR00T: a balanced mixture with robot_state at 37–50 and moderate vision_2. Any cell in the marked tier is defensible; picking the 104-episode argmax is not.
  • pi0.5: a vision_1-dominant mixture. Reusing GR00T's weights costs real success rate — the orderings barely correlate.
  • The pinned pick family gives no basis for a separate weight choice; it inherits the 13-task config.

Carry these caveats forward

  • These are pure-retrieval rates with always_hit. A warm-start run gates on a judge, so absolute levels here are not the levels you will see.
  • Differences below ~3 pp per cell are inside replay noise; the same (cell, task, seed) flips outcome 3.0 % of the time.
  • No latency evidence was collected in this round. text-IVF is supported as SR-neutral, not as cheaper.
