## 放宽与适配

1. π0.5/GR00T loaders: q3_port_1020/analysis.py as it is (imported, not edited): r16.analyze.require skips the "Changed judge" and "Changed client/policy protocol" checks, and its Reader fills vision=True and a policy call's miss_k (the shard's startup miss_steps) on decisions logged without them (long segment OFF serves without --os-blind, which is what logs them). Nothing else is relaxed; no shared loader was edited.
2. R17 whole_segment_result(selection, data, target, long_pure, ...): long_pure is the same-card C5 pure reference (target) itself. ON executes a task frozen at pure with fresh pure+long-segment (C10) episodes because its system runs C10; the OFF system runs C5 segments, so its pure fallback is the C5 reference. The labels "pure_long" in its output are renamed accordingly; native_length_fallback_diagnostic (identical to the final here) is dropped.
3. R17 measured_levels carry no RECALIBRATION.json predictions (those are the ON C10 decision-clock fits; OFF reuses the ON periodic_gap values without recalibration). They are descriptive only in ON.
4. Run dirs and references: FINAL_CHAINS_OFF.json maps canonical jobs to the dirs that hold their data and overrides two REFERENCES.json entries (listed there with reasons).
