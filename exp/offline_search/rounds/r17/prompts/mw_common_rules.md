- Goal (owner): fine-tune GR00T N1.5 and Fast-WAM on MetaWorld MT50 following the papers' recipes and measure every model's pure-inference success rate (GR00T-MW, Fast-WAM-MW, π0.5 RLinf, Cosmos-MW community). The coordinator runs long training/eval jobs; you build and verify tools.
- Ownership: A owns `exp/mw_finetune/` top level (evaluate.py, serve.py, adapters.py, aggregate.py, runtime.py, tests/) and MetaWorld env conventions (reuse `exp/metaworld/`). B owns `exp/mw_finetune/groot/`, C owns `exp/mw_finetune/fastwam/`, D owns `exp/mw_finetune/cosmos/`. Do not edit another agent's directory; if you need a change there, write a short request file `exp/mw_finetune/REQUESTS/<from>_to_<owner>.md` and continue with a local shim.

Until A publishes CONTRACT.md, follow exactly this.
- Data: `lerobot/metaworld_mt50` (2,500 eps, 204,806 frames, one 480² camera, 4-D state/action, fps 80). Local root `/data/mw_finetune/` (weilandserver), H100 root `/data/mw_finetune/` (≤ 120 GB total across agents; ~188 GB free now; keep ≥ 30 GB free).
