## A. What decides closed-loop success (paired, per init)

1. **Every failure is a step-cap timeout** (44 decisions spatial / 104 l10); successes finish at half the cap (sp 22.0 / 21.3 / 20.8 dec for CL0/1/2; l10 50.7 / 54.4 / 53.8). Nothing is "slow but eventually succeeds"; AWM is not faster on l10.
2. **Deadlocks decide the outcome.** Define a *spell* = ≥ 3 consecutive identical top-1 picks. P(success | no spell) = .97 / 1.00 / 1.00 (sp CL0/1/2) and .95 / .99 / .99 (l10). P(success | ≥ 2 spells) = .08 / .07 / .04 (sp), .10 / .20 / .34 (l10). 95–100 % of failed episodes contain a spell. Share of decisions inside spells: sp .20 / .22 / .19; **l10 .30 / .40 / .31** (M4 is the stickiest: 4.0 spells/episode vs B0 2.7, AWM 3.1). Within-arm AUROC(feature → success) for longest-run and stay-rate is .003–.08 (i.e. ≈ perfect inverse separation); visual stillness (`still` > 1.98, AWM extra) is .15–.22 of failed-episode decisions vs 0–.06 in successes.
3. **Why AWM wins on l10 (+19 pp) although offline err differs by .03**: it enters fewer spells (3.1 vs 4.0 per episode for M4), its spells are shorter (7.3 vs 8.2 / 8.9 decisions), and its escapes are productive: after a spell AWM advances +1.5 library steps in the same library episode (M4 +0.7, B0 +0.45) and 24 % of AWM's spells are in episodes that still succeed (M4 14 %, B0 10 %); P(success | exactly one spell) = .93 for AWM/M4 vs .72 for B0 on l10. Free (non-spell) decisions of failed episodes: 40 % for AWM vs 60–63 % for B0/M4. AWM agrees with the native B0 pick on 46 % / 41 % of decisions (38 % / 31 % in failed episodes).
4. **Spell taxonomy** (class of the FIRST spell of each failed episode; T = pick is the last row of its library episode, G = weighted gripper vote |v| < .5, Z = served translation norm < .6 σ, P = "pause row" = library row whose own next observation barely changed (top-decile stillness, 17 % / 14 % of rows), H = none of these):
   - spatial CL0/CL1/CL2: **T .67 / .62 / .38**, H .19 / .29 / .28, P .09 / .04 / .17, G – / .03 / .14, Z .05 / .02 / .03. Spells start at library progress .78–.88 with the gripper closed (83–91 %): the robot carries the bowl, lands on a terminal "hold" row (served tnorm 1.1 vs 1.67 free) and freezes.
   - l10: **H .61 / .54 / .47**, P .17 / .12 / .22, T .14 / .07 / .07, G – / .19 / .12, Z .09 / .08 / .11. Spells start mid-task (progress .62–.67), ~50 % gripper closed. Spell rows are enriched at library gripper transitions (within ±2 library steps of a gripper change: 43 / 56 / 48 % of spell rows vs 34–40 % of all served rows; 58 / 73 / 52 % for the first spell of failed episodes) → the traps are grasp/release events.
5. **Synthesis-specific trap = split-mode averaging at the grasp**: inside spells the set's gripper vote falls to |v| .75–.89 (free .91–.97), the mean's translation norm is 13–16 % below its members' (cancel .82–.87) and served |gripper| is .65–.75 → the robot hovers at grasp height with a 60/40 gripper (l10 task 3 example: 10 decisions at row 24.16, vote .2, tnorm .5, then closes). This is why **M4 loses its −.057 offline err on l10** (SR −1.2 pp): tasks 2/3/9 −.14/−.18/−.08, spell share on task 3 .50 vs B0 .21.
6. **AWM's spatial task-6 collapse (.72 → .28, 24 S→F flips; CL1 also .50)**: gripper chatter at the grasp (flips/episode 5.0 in failures vs 1.0 in successes; vote .67), the bowl is released/displaced, the scene leaves the library's distribution and the picker lands in a hub (row 28.8, open) forever. Offline, task 6 has AWM-fcur's highest vote-split rate (.158; M4 .050) whereas the *borrowed 500-episode fit* (`AWM_joint_cur_fbig`, same 50-ep candidates) has .044 and lower err (.586 vs .600) → **the 136-d per-task metric fitted on 111 rows is the culprit**, not the library. Fixing task 6 alone is worth ≈ +4.4 pp on spatial.
7. **CL3 spatial (V6 stuck-recovery + insurance over AWM) = .798 vs CL2 .800 (paired 25/24), task 6 .26.** Recovery fired on 11.3 % of decisions (30 % inside failed episodes, 1.3 % in successes), always changed the pick (identical-pick spells fell from 19 % to 4 % of decisions), yet 93 % of recovery decisions are in failed episodes and P(success | spell with recovery) = 0. Gripper flips/episode in failures went UP 2.9 → 4.9 despite hysteresis .4 (exclusion recovery switches trajectories and thereby gripper votes). **Conclusion: breaking the identical-pick loop is not escaping the trap.** After a failed grasp / displaced object the scene is off-library and no library action recovers → pure cache must PREVENT trap entry (P1–P3); a mixed system must MISS at trap entry (P4).

## B. Offline proxy screen — key negative deliverable

Computed on the trace store for B0, M4-k5-mean, AWM-kr5 (+ 8 variants) on `pi05_{spatial,l10}_cache`: err mean/p50/p90/worst-decile, regret, grip_mis (all and at teacher gripper transitions), err on B0-stuck decisions / failed episodes / late third, speed ratio ‖â‖/‖a_inf‖, member cancellation, gripper vote split, weight on terminal rows, action change inside stuck spells, top-1 stay rate. Target ordering: sp B0 .668 < M4 .764 < AWM .800; l10 M4 .428 ≈ B0 .440 ≪ AWM .630. **No proxy orders both suites.** err (and every err-derived proxy) orders spatial (+1.0) but says M4 ≫ B0 on l10 (.540 vs .597; closed loop −1.2 pp); grip_mis orders l10 (.173/.173/.168) but inverts spatial (.189/.206/.208); weight-on-terminal-rows orders spatial (.237/.146/.131) and half of l10. Per task, Spearman(ΔSR, Δerr) is **negative** (−.3 … −.7): tasks where offline err drops most are not where SR rises. Reason: the trace's states are B0's, while the SR-relevant events are method-specific dynamic traps at grasps (A.4–A.6). What the store *can* flag: the per-task vote-split rate (task 6 .158) and the fit gap fcur→fbig. **Recommendation for R3 screening**: stop using mean err as the gate; screen with a 100-episode closed-loop pilot per suite (20 inits × the 5 highest-trap tasks: sp {6, 9, 0, 4, 1}, l10 {0, 4, 6, 8, 7}; ~4 min wall each at the current throughput) and report the log KPIs that had AUROC ≥ .9 for episode failure: spells/episode, longest identical-pick run, gripper flips/episode, first-spell class shares, weight on terminal rows late. ## C. Proposals (ranked for pure-cache SR at the 50-episode library, then 10×; all keep AWM's vision-mandatory joint metric)

### P1 `awm_prior_metric` — AWM whose per-task metric is fitted with shrinkage to a big-library prior (candidates stay the 50 episodes)
1. Pitch: keep AWM_kr5 exactly, replace the 50-episode per-task whitening by (1−α)·S_w,task(current) + α·S_w,task(bpool 500 ep) (+ λ·tr/d·I), α ∈ {.5, 1}; same for the early (step-0) fit; PCA basis from the big pool (as `fit_data="big"` already does).
2. Hypothesis: with 74–125 rows per task (spatial) the 136-d metric is badly determined; neighbourhoods are then not action-consistent, so the kernel set straddles gripper modes at the grasp (task 6 vote-split .158 → chatter → OOD trap). α = 1 (`AWM(lib="current", fit_data="big")`, already coded and run offline) gives task-6 split .044, err .563/.491 vs .579/.513, weight on terminal rows .123/.035 vs .131/.053.
3. Algorithm (Method API, T1): in `fit`, open both libraries; for each task compute S_w on the current rows and on the big rows (same `fit_metric` pairs), M = (blend + λI)^-1; codes for the current candidates; everything else = AWM (kernel-16, kref 5, confidence, extras). α by LOEO on the current library (pseudo-query err) or fixed. Store per task W (136×136) — the fixed overhead the owner already accepted.
4. Predicted effect: offline stale err −.015/−.02 (measured at α = 1); closed loop spatial task 6 .28 → ≥ .7 ⇒ overall .80 → ≈ .84; l10 +2…+6 pp (fewer G/Z spells; risk: fbig's offline vote split is higher on two-object tasks 0/6/7, so α = .5 is the hedge). Three layers: synthesis unchanged, method layer = this, library layer = none (same 50 episodes). 10×: identical to `AWM_joint_big_fbig` (stale .499/.432) — nothing new needed.
5. Cost: 580 B/entry + 280 B action; current 1,018/2,640 entries → 0.9/2.3 MB + 17 MB PCA + 0.15 MB/task → 19–21 MB (deployed pkl 431/1,103 MB); 10× 27/43 MB. Query 1.1 ms; fit < 30 s.
6. Kill: closed-loop spatial task-6 SR < .6 or overall SR ≤ CL2 on both suites after 500 episodes.
7. Cheapest diagnostic: done offline (per-task split/err table in `a5.txt`); next = the 100-episode pilot on sp task 6 + l10 tasks 0/4/6.
8. Variants: α ∈ {.5, 1}; prior = big fit vs task-shared current fit; GR00T state_scale 3.

### P2 `grip_commit` — mode-consistent synthesis + gripper commitment (kwargs on AWM's `_insure`, T0)
1. Pitch: never let a split vote decide the gripper or dilute the translation.
2. Hypothesis: A.5/A.6 — the hover/chatter traps come from averaging across gripper modes; CL3 showed that hysteresis on the vote alone (.4) is insufficient when the selection itself switches trajectories.
3. Algorithm per decision: v = Σ w_i sign(g_i) at executed step 0. (a) Served sign = sign of v if |v| ≥ .8, else the previously served sign (step 0: top-1's sign). (b) A sign change vs the previous served sign requires |v| ≥ .8 on this AND the previous decision (2-decision confirmation) and no change in the last 3 decisions (min dwell; forbids open→close→open within 3 decisions). (c) Translation/rotation = kernel mean over the members of the *served* gripper class only (renormalized weights; falls back to all members if the class has < 3 members) — removes the cancellation (cancel .82–.87 in spells). Per-episode state: last served sign, decisions since last change. Extras: `gvote, gclass_w, gdwell, gheld`.
4. Predicted: offline err +.00…+.01 (mode-consistent mean is closer to the teacher's mode), grip_mis unchanged; closed loop: flips/episode in failed episodes 2.9–5.0 → ≤ 2; spatial task 6 → ≥ .6 (with P1 the two fixes overlap); l10 G+Z first-spell classes (.19–.23) partly removed → +3…+6 pp for AWM, +5…+10 pp for M4-type selectors. Layers: synthesis-layer effect only; works at any library size (10× identical code).
5. Cost: 0 bytes, µs.
6. Kill: flips/episode in failures not below 2, or SR on task 6 not above .5, or offline grip_mis at teacher transitions +> .02.
7. Diagnostic: count offline decisions with |v| < .8 at teacher gripper transitions (proxy of how often the rule intervenes); then the pilot.
8. Variants: threshold {.6, .8}; dwell {2, 3}; class-consistent mean on/off.

### P3 `terminal_guard` — keep the cache off terminal "hold" rows until the task can be finished (T0)
1. Pitch: spatial B0 first spells are 67 % terminal-row absorption (AWM 38 %); the terminal row of a library episode has no successor and its action is "hold".
2. Hypothesis: near the end, the query state matches terminal rows best although the task is not done (bowl slightly off the plate); serving "hold" freezes the observation → fixed point. Excluding those rows makes the served action the pre-terminal "lower & release" one.
3. Algorithm: candidate mask per decision: drop rows with lib_step ≥ ep_len−2 unless (step ≥ .9·median library episode length of the task AND the served gripper has already opened in this episode); when the mask empties the task set, relax. Different mechanism from the settled "step alignment" negative (that re-weighted all rows by phase; this removes 4.8 % / 1.9 % of rows in one situation).
4. Predicted: offline err ±.005 (terminal rows have ordinary norms, tnorm 2.7); closed loop spatial +3…+6 pp for B0-like selectors, +2…+4 pp for AWM (its T share is .38 of first spells); l10 +1…+2 pp. Layers: method (candidate set); library unchanged; at 10× the T share is the same fraction, so the effect persists.
5. Cost: 1 bit/entry.
6. Kill: no reduction of terminal-class first spells in the pilot or offline err +> .01.
7. Diagnostic: offline `w_term_late` (.27–.48 now) with/without the mask + err on late decisions; then the spatial pilot.
8. Variants: last 1 vs 2 rows; gate on progress only.

### P4 `trap_miss_trigger` — for the mixed HIT/MISS system (FINDINGS Q3): MISS on trap entry, not on low err
1. Pitch: the SR-relevant confidence is "am I entering a trap", which the per-decision err confidence does not measure (B).
2. Hypothesis: traps are unrecoverable under pure cache (CL3: P(success | spell with recovery) = 0) but a single policy call *before* the failed grasp / at the first frozen decision returns the robot to the teacher's distribution.
3. Algorithm (closed-loop plugin: judge respects the method's confidence; confidence = −trap score): trap score = max of (i) gripper-transition ambiguity: a sign change is proposed with |v| < .8, (ii) no-progress: 2 consecutive decisions with both-camera stillness ≥ 95th library percentile AND state motion ≤ 10th percentile (V6's detector, already logged as `still`/`stuck_n`), (iii) terminal pick while the gripper is closed, (iv) picked library progress not advancing for 3 decisions. Threshold chosen for a target MISS rate.
4. Predicted: in CL3 the stuck flag fired on 11 % of decisions, 30 % inside failed episodes vs 1.3 % in successes → a trigger at ~5–10 % MISS rate should convert a large share of the 100–185 failed episodes per suite; report SR vs inference ratio (owner's definition). Layers: orthogonal to synthesis/method/library.
5. Cost: 0 bytes; needs stage 2/3 on the server (allowed for R3).
6. Kill: MISS rate > 20 % for < +5 pp SR, or triggers fire mostly after the grasp has already failed (check timing: first trigger step vs first spell step in the CL2/CL3 logs).
7. Diagnostic (offline on existing logs, no new runs): for CL2/CL3 failed episodes, the step of the first trigger vs the first spell start; fraction of successes that would have been interrupted.
8. Variants: (i)+(ii) only; 1 vs 2 confirmation decisions.

**Ranking**: P1 (largest measured lever at the 50-episode library, one kwarg on an existing method, fixes the task-6 collapse) > P2 (attacks the mechanism that also sank M4 on l10; zero cost; combine with P1 in one arm and as an ablation) > P3 (spatial-specific, largest for B0-like selectors) > P4 (the right direction for the real system, but needs the mixed server). Recommended R3 closed-loop arms at 50 episodes: {CL2 reference, P1(α=1), P1+P2, P1+P2+P3}; at 10×: {AWM_big, AWM_big+P2+P3}. Not proposed: trajectory-coherence-by-blend (V6's `next` blend was on in CL3, w_nx .22, nx_in 79 %, no SR gain), more exclusion-type recovery, and any fix that only changes offline err.
