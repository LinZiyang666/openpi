## Proposals (ranked by expected closed-loop value)

### P1. `v7awm_mixed_judge` — predicted-err judge on AWM, guards → MISS, one threshold, quantile-matched hit rate
1. **Pitch.** HIT iff V7-on-AWM predicted err ≤ τ and no drift guard fires; τ set by a running quantile so the realized hit rate equals the target in every arm (deployment: the frozen τ the controller converges to).
2. **Hypothesis.** The two things a mixed judge must do are (i) rank states by expected error on one scale across the fresh and stale regimes and (ii) hand the lost states to the policy. F2/F3 show the predicted-err scale is the only one of the three (B0 score, AWM own, V7) that is both regime-aware and shared; F5–F7 show the guards fire on the states with err .6–.9 and inside failing episodes; F8 shows the absolute threshold cannot be set offline for a selector whose own loop visits different states — the hit rate must be matched online.
3. **Algorithm.** Server plugin, full model (`--stage2-device cuda:0 --stage3-device cuda:0`, STAGE1_ONLY=0). Method = `g3_recovery/wrappers.py:DriftCalibratedConfidence(base=awm.py:AWM, base_kwargs={"lib":"current","kref":5} | {} for 10×, ot_still=True)`; `Result.confidence = −pred_err + 1e-6·zsum` (already implemented, bit-exact online = offline per the R2 evidence). Judge (new `PluginJudge` mode `--os-judge`): MISS if `extras.stuck == 1` (V7 exposes stuck_n / terminal / overtime / lag / stuck) else HIT iff `confidence ≥ τ_t`. `τ_t` = the (1−h)-quantile of the confidences of the last W = 1000 decisions handled by this server (all connections; guarded decisions included with −∞ so they consume miss budget), initialised from the offline mixture table (F4) and kept running for the whole arm; the deployment value is the median τ_t over the second half of the run (report it). Bookkeeping: on MISS the plugin records `hits[step] = 0` and the policy's executed chunk (already delivered by `Interceptor.broadcast_action → strategy.record_action` after inference; `on_executed` must stop hard-coding `hits.append(1)` and instead use the judge's verdict; `exec_ok` then compares against the policy chunk). Log per decision: hit, τ_t, pred_err, zsum, guard flags, `pre_ms` (stage 1) and stage-2/3 ms. Calibration data: library LOEO (deployable, no trace); optional trace calibration (owner §9.2 allows) as variant.
4. **Predicted effect.** Offline mixture (F4): accepted err .27/.26/.28/.29 at h = .5 (current; B0 .375/.365/.401/.422), .35/.36/.33/.37 at h = .7 (B0 .43/.42/.43/.47); 10×: .21/.22/.22/.23 and .27/.30/.27/.31. Bad-HIT rate (π0.5) .34/.28 vs B0 .57/.46 at h = .5. Closed loop (priors, not results): every failing episode gets policy intervention by ~1/3 of its length at h = .7 (F6), so SR(h = .7) should sit much closer to pure inference (.986/.844/.940/.870) than to pure cache (.800/.630/—/—): π0.5-sp .90–.96 (B0 .85–.93), π0.5-l10 .72–.80 (B0 .60–.72); at h = .5 π0.5-sp .95–.98 / l10 .78–.83 (B0 .92–.96 / .70–.78). The AWM−B0 gap should be largest on l10 (F4 gap + F6 earlier firing) and shrink to ≈ 0 at h ≤ .3 (70 % of decisions are after-MISS, both judges accept the easy ones), growing to the pure-cache gap (+13/+19 pp) at h → 1.
5. **Cost / tier.** T1. +46 B/entry, <10 KB maps, 2.6 MB task means per suite; wrapper +1.1 ms/query on top of AWM's ~1–2 ms (single thread); controller O(1) per decision (sorted ring buffer). Fit 3–9 s current / 17–39 s 10× (prefit artifact, `OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1`).
6. **Kill.** At matched h = .5 and .7 on π0.5-l10, SR(AWM judge) − SR(B0 judge) ≤ +2 pp; or the periodic-refresh control (P2) matches P1 within 2 pp at the same h on both π0.5 suites (targeting adds nothing).
7. **Cheapest diagnostic.** Done offline (F2–F5, F8). Online: the first 50 episodes of the h = .5 arm — check realized h within ±3 pp of target and the guard share among MISSes ≥ 40 %.
8. **Variants.** fixed offline τ vs controller (measures offline→online drift, F8 predicts +10–15 pp); per-task τ (allocate the inference budget where SR is lowest: l10 tasks 0/6/8 have B0 SR 10–14 %, task 5 88 %; diagnostic = `profile.breakdown --slices task,conf_dec`); guards on/off; trace-calibrated vs library-LOEO maps; W = 300 vs 1000.

### P2. `refresh_cap_and_periodic_control` — bounded HIT runs + the blind periodic-refresh control arm
1. **Pitch.** Force a MISS after R consecutive HITs (R = 8), and run a judge-free control that MISSes every k-th decision at the same hit rate.
2. **Hypothesis.** F9: staleness penalty is ≈ 0 for runs ≤ 3, +.06 at 8, +.14 at 12, +.25 at 16. A run cap bounds the after-HIT penalty regardless of the judge's blind spots (F10: early parts of failing episodes look confident). The periodic arm separates "any policy intervention at rate 1−h" from "targeted intervention" — without it a mixed-mode SR gain cannot be attributed to the judge.
3. **Algorithm.** Cap: in the judge, `run ≥ R → MISS` (run = consecutive HITs, reset on MISS); counts toward the controller's miss budget. Periodic control: HIT unless `step mod k == k−1` (k = 3 → h ≈ .67, k = 2 → .5), AWM selector, no confidence used.
4. **Predicted effect.** Cap R = 8 at h = .7: removes the s > 8 tail (≈ 10–15 % of after-HIT decisions in the cache cells, err .5–.7) at a cost of ≤ 1/R ≈ 12 % of decisions, mostly ones the judge would have missed anyway → expect ≤ .01 mixed err change, SR effect only visible if P1 leaves late deadlocks (l10). Periodic control: expected SR between pure cache and P1; the P1 − periodic gap at matched h is the value of targeting (my prior: +3–8 pp on l10 at h = .7, ≈ 0–3 pp on spatial).
5. **Cost.** T0, zero bytes, one counter.
6. **Kill.** Cap: SR(P1 + cap) − SR(P1) ≤ 0 at h = .7 on l10. Control: if periodic ≥ P1 − 2 pp, the judge line is dead and the deliverable curve is set by the selector alone.
7. **Cheapest diagnostic.** Offline: mixed err vs R using F9's run-length curve (already tabulated in `judge_tables.md` "Staleness penalty").
8. **Variants.** R ∈ {6, 8, 12}; periodic k ∈ {2, 3}; periodic with B0 selector (isolates selector vs judge fully).

### P3. `shadow_teacher_labels` — teacher chunks on HIT decisions in the mixed loop → measured accepted err and pass-2 recalibration (π0.5 only)
1. **Pitch.** Enable the existing X15 `shadow_teacher` (CP1, π0.5) in the pass-1 mixed arms: one extra teacher forward per HIT, recorded not executed, isolated RNG; gives err for every HIT decision of the mixed loop.
2. **Hypothesis.** R2's central complaint is that offline err does not map to closed-loop SR (FINDINGS: "not proportional"). The missing quantity is the accepted-HIT err *in the mixed loop's own state distribution*; the offline mixture is a bound (F9). With labels: (i) direct offline→online check of F4, (ii) isotonic recalibration of the V7 maps on mixed-regime states (the fresh/stale mixture the deployment actually visits), (iii) the SR-relevant risk model (bad HITs per episode → failure) fitted on real episodes.
3. **Algorithm.** Yaml `shadow_teacher: {enabled: true, path: <sidecar.jsonl>, action_sigma: <σ_d from library current>}` (mutually exclusive with `trace.enabled`, which is off in the closed-loop yamls; `not supported on CP2` — fine, CP1 only). Sidecar rows carry `teacher_chunk` (fp16) and `u`; join on (task_uid, attempt, decision_idx) with the plugin decision log → err per HIT. Pass 2: refit the three isotonic maps (step 0 / fresh / stale) on the labelled pass-1 decisions (episode-parity cross-fit for reporting), keep the z-scales; rerun the h = .5 arms.
4. **Predicted effect.** Measurement, not err change; pass-2 recalibration expected to move accepted err by ≤ .01 but to fix the realized-rate drift of fixed τ (F8) and to give the deployable frozen τ.
5. **Cost.** T1 for the maps; GPU: doubles stage-2/3 work on HITs → arm wall ≈ pure-inference wall (sp ≈ 10 min, l10 ≈ 25 min) — only on the h = .5 π0.5 arms. Not available for GR00T (no `sample_noise` seam; `grep` finds no groot support).
6. **Kill.** Sidecar error rate > 5 % or any executed-action change (the recorder must be byte-neutral: compare the h = .5 arm SR with and without shadow on 100 episodes; > 2 pp difference = kill).
7. **Cheapest diagnostic.** Smoke one server with `shadow_teacher.enabled` on 5 episodes and check `status == ok` rows = n_decisions and `exec_ok` unchanged.
8. **Variants.** Label every HIT vs a 30 % random subsample of HITs (cost control); use labels to fit per-task τ (P1 variant).

### P4. `guard_only_judge` — no confidence threshold: HIT unless a drift guard fires
1. **Pitch.** The zero-calibration operating point: MISS on stuck / terminal / overtime-and-lagging states only; hit rate emerges (≈ .70–.79).
2. **Hypothesis.** F7: 83–85 % of the guard's MISSes fall in failing episodes although those hold only 35–53 % of decisions; the guard's thresholds are library statistics (10th-pct motion, 95th-pct consecutive-key cosine, median episode length), so nothing is fitted on traces and the judge is identical for every selector. This is the deployment most likely to be accepted if the owner wants "no threshold to tune".
3. **Algorithm.** P1's plugin judge with `τ = −∞` and the V6 detector (`ot_still=True`, `stuck_thr=2`, `lag_thr=5`) → MISS. Optional: add P2's run cap R = 12 as the only other rule.
4. **Predicted effect.** Hit rate ≈ .79 (sp) / .70 (l10) (F7, without the stuck clause; with it ≈ .75 / .68), IR ≈ .33 / .41. Accepted err ≈ the "unguarded" stale err .49/.49/.40/.49 (current) blended with fresh ≈ .34 → ≈ .45 mixed, i.e. worse than P1 at equal h (P1 at h = .7: .35/.36/.33/.37) but with all lost states removed. SR prior: between P1(h = .7) and pure cache; on l10 closer to P1 because the failures are deadlocks (F6/F7).
5. **Cost.** T0; +46 B/entry (V6 tables), 2.6 MB task means; +1.3 ms/query.
6. **Kill.** SR(guard-only) < SR(P1 at the same realized h) − 5 pp on both π0.5 suites.
7. **Cheapest diagnostic.** Done (F7). Online: guard share of MISSes in failing episodes ≥ 70 %.
8. **Variants.** with/without the terminal clause (22 % of stale decisions on spatial — many are legitimate end-of-task states in successful episodes: F5 shows terminal err .87 pooled, but the successful-episode subset is untested; log it separately); R = 12 cap on.

**Not proposed (measured).** A calibrated judge over B0's inputs (F1: AURC .403 vs .414 — no lever). Library-side recovery in mixed mode (F11). Fixed offline τ as the primary protocol (F8). Success-only libraries, flip-aware synthesis, etc. remain dead per R2.
