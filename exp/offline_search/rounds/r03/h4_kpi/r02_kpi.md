## 5. Caveats

* Served actions are reconstructed from the logged top-10 rows / scores (AWM's kernel has 16 members: ≈ .03 σ RMS error, ideation C; G3
  wrappers use the base-contract weights on the logged set). Gripper signs and votes are robust to this; `tnorm`-based Z classes are borderline
  cases by construction (.6 σ threshold).
* Mixed-mode fields (`hit`, `judge`, `src`, `tau`, `run`, `s1_ms`, `s23_ms`, `os_force_miss`, `os_reason`, logged policy chunk) are validated on a
  synthetic arm built from real CL2 rows (every 4th decision a MISS), not on H2's real logs, which do not exist yet; field names follow the H4
  brief and are tolerated when absent. Spells / flips / taxonomy are computed on HIT decisions only; "first MISS vs first spell" uses the
  proposal spells (all decisions) and the served spells.
* `ir_formula_pi05` is the project definition for π0.5 only; for GR00T use `--ir-ref` / `--ir-ref-ms` (measured ratio) or the stage-split
  `ir_stage_measured` once `s1_ms` / `s23_ms` are logged.
* The pause-row mask follows A's definition on each served library (top-decile visual stillness or bottom-decile state motion, non-terminal);
  on a 10× library it costs ~10 s per arm (`--cache-dir` caches it).
