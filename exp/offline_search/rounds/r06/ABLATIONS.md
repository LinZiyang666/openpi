- **No-progress is the only guard whose removal is detectable.** On both LIBERO-10-50 cells it carries all of B's
  gain: without it SR returns to A (π0.5 .712 vs A .714, GR00T .620 vs A .611; n.s. against every A replicate). On
  π0.5 Spatial-50 it carries ≈ 5 of B's ≈ 7 pp.
- **On GR00T Spatial-50 the same guard is harmful.** Removing it raises SR to .906, above every B replicate
  (p ≤ .015) and every A replicate (p ≤ .001), while lowering IR (.147 → .105).
- **The other three guards** (stuck, terminal, overtime) are individually removable in all four cells without a
  detectable SR change. Removing terminal also saves IR on π0.5 Spatial-50 (.140 → .124) and GR00T Spatial-50
  (.147 → .131). Joint removal is not tested here; configuration C tests it.
- **Mechanism** (Bmech arms, `r06_method`, ledger 09-29 08:51–09:07): disabling only the no-progress *MISS bit* while
  keeping its blind-LOOK veto matches "no-progress removed" in all four cells (exact p = .75–1.0 after the repair). The guard's effect, good or
  bad, is entirely through the policy takeover it triggers, not through observation cadence.
