## Plain reading

1. **Budget (Q2's ρ) is the knob that matters.** Realized IR tracks ρ within ±.02 in 26 of 28 arms, using only the
   library and ≤ 10 non-test recordings per cell.
2. **Where to spend (Q1's R) did not matter at these budgets.** Calls placed where predicted cache–policy disagreement
   is high did no better than uniformly placed calls. R predicts disagreement, not recoverable failure.
3. **A calibrated stall signal is the useful placement signal.** It keeps all of the benefit of B's hand-set
   no-progress guard on the long-task libraries. It turns that guard's harm on GR00T Spatial-50 into a gain, and it
   adds 2–3 pp on both GR00T cells at equal IR. The Bmech arms show why this is the right level: the guard acts
   entirely through policy takeover.
4. **C should not spend on libraries that are already good enough.** On the dense Spatial-500 libraries, extra calls
   are neutral or harmful. GR00T Sp-500 C18 is below pure cache. A library-level "no-call" gate, for example cache
   quality from the calibration recordings, is the missing piece.
5. **At high budget, the task-level risk lottery beats C in two of three cells.** No U45 arm was run, so the cause is
   not isolated. It could be task-level allocation, or C's cooldowns and extra LOOKs, which spend budget.
