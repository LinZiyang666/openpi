**6. Data we want but do not have**

Owner follow-up: requirements for the single superset profiler, **not a profiler implementation or a new experiment launch**. Priority is Q1: predicting A’s library-dependent SR, its gap to inference, and B’s gain, globally/per task/per state. The planned shadow chunk, randomized MISS, retrieval/guard internals, snapshots and outcome joins are accepted as the baseline. Below, **covered** means the stated plan can supply the quantity if its schema is complete; **extension** means it is not guaranteed by that plan; **outside one rollout** means no additional logging field alone can provide it. These collection targets do not replace §4’s eight-arm test plan.

The most important distinction is between **a second proposed action**, **a second observed transition**, and **a second complete-policy outcome**. The shadow chunk supplies the first. Randomized execution supplies factual transitions for both treatments over a population. A valid saved state plus later branch execution can supply paired transitions/outcomes from the same state. None of these by itself supplies pure-A SR on states reached under a different, repeatedly randomized controller.

Existing-data qualification: this is not a claim that all low-level fields are absent everywhere. `harness/store.py:QueryCell.ARRAYS` already defines keys, state and executed chunks for old recordings; `closed_loop/plugin.py:_write_inputs` can optionally save them, including blind-execution information. The sampled R5 A decision/client/journal schemas are preserved in `data_wishlist_existing_fields.json`; that run has decision records and a served head, but no complete every-anchor shadow/alternative-successor bundle. The existing K5 randomization is restricted to guard landmarks, π0.5 l10, and its old controller (`../../r05/q3_callvalue/HANDBACK.md`). The missing product is a complete, consistently joined dataset under the target A/committed-MISS execution, plus the counterfactual and independent-validation quantities below. Preserve an already-recorded field; do not duplicate it merely because it appears in this list.

**Precision conventions for every ranked item.** All requested sample sizes below are **planning targets/assumptions, not measurements of a new run or guarantees of power**. A base cell means model × suite; a bank cell additionally fixes library contents, fit, and controller. The current first-pass coverage target is the eight endpoint bank cells: both models × both suites × nominal 50/500. Anchors from one episode are dependent, and repeated seeds of the same task/init are paired, not new independent initializations.

- For one success probability, the normal-approximation planning rule is `n ≥ 1.96² p(1−p)/h²`: a 95% half-width of 5 pp needs 385 independent episodes at worst-case p=.5, or 139 assuming p=.9; 3 pp needs 1,068 or 385, respectively. Use Wilson/clustered intervals in the actual report. Fifty episodes **per task** do not give precise task-level SR or intervention effects.
- For a paired binary contrast with discordance fraction d and mean contrast near zero, half-width is approximately `1.96 sqrt(d/n)`. At 500 paired episodes and assumed d=.10–.30 it is **2.77–4.80 pp**; at 1,000 it is **1.96–3.39 pp**. At 50 task-level pairs it is **8.77–15.18 pp**. A 500-episode cell can screen substantial effects but cannot generally settle a 1–2 pp quality ranking.
- For a balanced, **unpaired** randomized binary-outcome contrast, the worst-case 95% half-width is about `1.96/sqrt(N)`, requiring approximately **385 / 1,537 / 4,269 independent episode contributions for 10 / 5 / 3 pp**, respectively. Those counts apply to the particular quality stratum being estimated. Repeated anchor outcomes, propensity weighting and rare eligible states can reduce effective precision; 1,537 anchors are not 1,537 independent episode contributions. Log denominators and estimate episode-clustered uncertainty rather than claiming a fixed anchor-count guarantee.
- For continuous residuals, use 30 pilot episodes per bank cell to estimate the SD of an **episode-level** residual summary, then plan `n ≥ (1.96 s_episode/h)²` at the desired half-width h. For conditional estimates, do this within broad predeclared quality strata; start with three strata rather than a task × phase × guard × score cross-product.
- A rough Fisher-transform planning approximation gives about **47 independent library/domain evaluation units** for 80% power to distinguish correlation .4 from zero at a two-sided .05 threshold (30 for .5; 85 for .3). This is not an exact Spearman/clustered power result. More trajectories for the same eight banks reduce SR noise, but do not create independent library-quality examples.

These arithmetic calculations and measured old anchor counts are reproducible with:

```bash
taskset -c 10-13,54-57 env OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 MKL_NUM_THREADS=1 CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES='' PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE=1 .venv/bin/python exp/offline_search/rounds/r06/ideation_Q1/data_wishlist_precision.py
```

Outputs: `data_wishlist_precision.json`; source of anchor counts: this directory’s `source_index.json` and its audited per-arm summaries. The same episodes can serve several items below; **do not add all suggested counts together**. A precision shortfall should be reported as unresolved, not filled by treating additional anchors as independent episodes.

**Rank 1 — The exact factual transition associated with each candidate/shadow pair.**

- **What / gap:** A loss-audited record of the observation that generated both chunks; the actual action sequence applied to the simulator/robot; and the states reached after one decision and after the full commitment. The plan covers proposed chunks and retrieval internals, but does not explicitly guarantee application-time action transforms, completion/truncation masks, terminal observations, or complete raw retrieval inputs. A logged full chunk is not proof that its tail was executed. This is an **extension/schema guarantee**, with some fields already available in older optional dumps.
- **Granularity / Q1 use:** Per anchor, linked to every executed control and successor observation, plus a per-library/per-run schema. This makes action error, actual successor error, coverage breaks and state-dependent quality under the served controller well-defined. Save unprojected stage-1 keys, robot state, instruction/task identity, camera/preprocessing/encoder identity, valid coordinate masks/units, frame and control timestamps, library/fit/checkpoint hashes, normalized kernel weights/row identities, pre-transform chunk, post-transform commands, controls actually completed, and next-anchor/terminal links. Reuse shared metadata by reference. Save applied actions after clipping/rescaling, and distinguish proposal, wire payload and actual execution. Missing records must have explicit flags and reasons, not silently shifted row joins.
- **Measurement / marginal cost:** Capture existing arrays and actuator/simulator acknowledgements; read state at the intermediate boundary and immediately after the committed block, including early termination. No additional policy inference. Raw keys must be sufficient to replay other PCA/metric fits offline; only the deployed PCA code/top-k is insufficient. In this installation two float32 32,768-dimensional keys cost **262,144 bytes = 256 KiB per anchor**, before compression. Existing A endpoints have 5,449–18,218 anchors per 500 episodes, giving **1.33–4.45 GiB** of uncompressed key payload per such cell. This is a storage estimate from old counts, not the new randomized rollout’s measured size. State/action metadata are smaller; exact logging latency must be measured, not assumed zero. RGB/depth is optional for this fixed encoder but needed if later testing a different visual encoder.
- **Episodes/cells:** Capture every eligible anchor in all eight endpoint bank cells. **500 episodes/cell, balanced over tasks**, is an initial shared collection target, not precise per-task SR calibration. Use the continuous-residual pilot rule above to decide whether error calibration needs more episodes. The resulting inputs also permit offline rescoring of all compatible library/fit variants without running their searches online.

**Rank 2 — Quality-conditioned causal CACHE-versus-MISS outcomes with actual treatment support.**

- **What / gap:** For pre-action Q and its components, estimate the difference in later success/short-horizon transition quality caused by choosing the shadow policy chunk instead of the cache chunk. This is **mostly covered by the planned randomization**, unlike the existing broad observational correlations; what must be guaranteed is the propensity of the **actual served treatment**, not just the injection coin. Log eligibility, pre-coin guard verdict, random draw/probability, forced overrides, actual treatment, policy/commit length, and sampling/missingness probabilities. A guard that always forces MISS creates no CACHE support in that stratum even if an injection coin was drawn.
- **Granularity / Q1 use:** Per anchor linked to the complete episode treatment history and outcome. It tests whether “low library quality” predicts benefit from inference, rather than merely large imitation error. Use pre-treatment score/progress estimates; do not stratify causal effects using final normalized episode progress, later guard states, or a failure selected after the intervention.
- **Measurement / marginal cost:** Use the existing planned shadow draw for served MISS and store the complete assignment/compliance record. **No extra inference beyond the planned shadow calculation** and small scalar-log overhead. Keep randomization independent of policy sampling and log the future controller policy/version. Sequential randomization identifies an excursion effect under that specified randomized continuation and its visited-state distribution; it does not automatically identify an individual state’s two outcomes or A/B whole-policy SR.
- **Episodes/cells:** Begin with the same **500 episodes in each of the eight endpoint cells** and three broad predeclared quality strata, pooling tasks initially. Require both actual treatments and report cluster-effective support. This is exploratory precision: a 5 pp outcome-effect half-width can need about **1,537 independent episode contributions per stratum** under the conservative calculation above, and more with weak support. Do not promise task-specific causal tables from 50 episodes/task. Q1 needs these labels; choosing a MISS controller from them remains Q2/Q3’s work.

**Rank 3 — Paired successor and success labels for unserved actions, with validated snapshot restoration.**

- **What / gap:** `next_state(cache)` versus `next_state(policy)` and final success after either choice **from the same anchor**, with an explicitly identical continuation rule afterward. Neither a shadow action nor the factual randomized outcome contains the unchosen successor. Periodic simulator snapshots are **only partly sufficient**: we still need an eligible anchor’s restorable state and later branch executions. A simulator qpos/qvel dump alone is not necessarily a complete experiment state.
- **Granularity / Q1 use:** Per sampled anchor/branch, clustered by its parent episode, with branch-level and final outcome labels. Store simulator/environment state, task progress/termination state, controller/actuator state, random-generator states, pending action/commit buffers, retrieval/guard history, and any policy temporal state required to resume the specified continuation. Include simulator/model/config versions and a replay checksum. This distinguishes density/teacher agreement from physical action competence and turns successor prediction into a matched intervention comparison.
- **Measurement / marginal cost:** During collection, save complete checkpoints at a random or explicitly stratified set of anchors, including high-confidence anchors, with inclusion probabilities and snapshot failures. Periodic-only capture can alias the relevant phase; guard-only capture cannot measure confident failures. Online cost is serialization/I/O and whatever state-copy synchronization restoration requires, not another policy forward pass. A restore-then-replay-the-same-action check must compare control-boundary state and termination with the original; record drift/tolerance rather than declaring feasibility from file existence. Later run both cached and saved-shadow chunks for exactly the same completed-control horizon, then use a named frozen continuation policy to termination. Two H-control branches cost **2H simulated controls per checkpoint**; full continuations and any later policy calls are additional, not free. A continuation using only A avoids policy calls but estimates a different target from B or pure inference.
- **Episodes/cells:** For feasibility, **100 checkpoints from 100 distinct episodes** spread over the four base cells; this tests restoration, not action value. With zero replay failures, the one-sided 95% binomial upper failure rate is still **2.95%**; 300 independent successes lower it below **1%**. For value screening, save **200–500 distinct parent episodes/cell**, initially one evaluated anchor per episode, balanced over tasks and coarse Q strata. At 500 parents the paired-success precision remains about **2.8–4.8 pp** under the stated discordance assumptions; fine task/phase effects need more parents. Capturing restorable snapshots now preserves the option to pay branch cost later. If restoration is infeasible, explicitly forgo same-state paired labels; do not substitute a visually similar state.

**Rank 4 — Matched complete-policy occupancy and outcomes for A, B and pure inference.**

- **What / gap:** The joint distribution of quality/state trajectories and outcome under **unmodified A**, exact B, and a declared inference commitment length. Existing arms provide endpoint SRs, but not the new complete shadow/transition dataset on their actual trajectories. The profiler’s repeatedly randomized MISS stream has its own occupancy distribution. This is **outside extra logging on a single mixed-policy rollout**.
- **Granularity / Q1 use:** Per episode and per bank, with anchor records from rank 1. Needed for absolute A SR prediction, the gap to pure inference, and B−A gain. Reweighting a long sequence of repeated coin flips into an all-CACHE trajectory can have negligible effective support; recording propensities does not fix that automatically.
- **Measurement / marginal cost:** In the single collection program, reserve predeclared whole-episode strata for unchanged A, exact B and pure inference, with paired task/init identities, run seeds and fixed commit semantics. Continue shadow logging in those strata. An A stratum has actual MISS probability zero; do not pretend its anchors are randomized treatment comparisons. A pure-inference reference must declare H (10 controls for the direct commitment-matched comparison here); an L5 reference answers a different question. Additional episodes cost rollouts; serving the already-shadowed policy chunk adds no policy forward call at that anchor. Endpoint data cannot be manufactured by relabeling the mixed stream.
- **Episodes/cells:** **500 paired initializations per bank endpoint comparison** initially; **1,000** if distinguishing differences on the order of several pp is important, then assess seed variation separately. Pure inference can be shared across libraries within a fixed model/suite/controller contract. Under a tighter budget, retain fewer well-covered endpoint bank cells instead of claiming that mixed-stream outcomes estimate pure-A SR. Existing endpoints remain historical references, not independent validation of a newly fitted Q→SR map.

**Rank 5 — Independent library-content and fit variation at fixed cardinality.**

- **What / gap:** New bank-level outcome examples that separate episode count, collection source, success filtering, representation fit and content quality. The current nested size curve and two GR00T endpoints do not provide that separation. Logging every retrieval on one bank does not create outcomes for another bank. This requires **additional bank variation**, not an extra anchor field.
- **Granularity / Q1 use:** Per library/fit and its evaluation episodes. It tests whether Q predicts SR beyond library size and whether its global/task rankings survive a held-out library. Preserve complete content/fit manifests, selection seed, corpus/episode hashes, overlap graph, fit/normalization provenance, admission rules and evaluation split. Much of this exists for current banks; the missing quantity is replicated variation and its matched outcomes.
- **Measurement / marginal cost:** Construct candidate subsets/fits offline; serve a frozen bank selected at episode start and log that selection probability/identity. Replay all alternative banks on saved rank-1 keys offline for comparable fixed-state diagnostics. That CPU replay gives alternative proposals, **not their closed-loop SRs**. Obtain selected banks’ outcomes through actual episode assignment or properly replayed branches. No additional policy forward is required for another bank’s offline retrieval, but actual bank-conditioned trajectories cost additional episodes.
- **Episodes/cells:** For a useful preliminary content/size check, target **three independently seeded subset draws at each of two sizes in each of the four base cells: 24 bank/fit evaluation units**, with **200 episodes/unit for screening and 500 for confirmation**. Draws from the same finite corpus may overlap and are conditional on that corpus; they are not independent acquisition populations. Include a matched frozen/refit contrast where feasible. These are data-adequacy targets for the coordinator, not 24 newly proposed deployment arms or a claim that one eight-bank pass has global-rank precision. The approximate 47-independent-unit calculation above explains why even 24 dependent units cannot establish a general correlation reliably. Never replace independent bank units by thousands of anchors from one fit.

**Rank 6 — Task-relevant physical changes and failure/milestone labels.**

- **What / gap:** Object motion/pose/velocity, object–robot contact or attachment changes, task predicate changes, first success time, loss of an achieved predicate, and categorized terminal failures/truncations. These are not generally in the action-cache store’s 8-D proprioception, and “all guard inputs” does not guarantee them. A final success bit is already planned; it does not explain why a small/large residual mattered. This is a **privileged diagnostic extension**.
- **Granularity / Q1 use:** Per control or decision boundary, plus per-episode labels. It tests the loss mismatch behind weak SR ranking, provides physical successor labels, and distinguishes task progress from the hindsight fraction of elapsed episode length. Preserve observation-available versus simulator-only flags; these labels must not silently enter a deployable robot score.
- **Measurement / marginal cost:** Read simulator state/contact/task predicates at the same execution boundaries, store stable object/predicate IDs and the schema/units, plus occasional RGB/depth for review. No extra VLA inference. Physics/renderer access, serialization and any human annotation still have a cost; measure them in the pilot. For another benchmark/robot, use its sensors/evaluator and mark unavailable fields, rather than hard-coding LIBERO object names or a gripper sign. A curated failure taxonomy can be computed afterward from these raw records.
- **Episodes/cells:** Collect cheap state/predicate channels in **all eight endpoint bank cells**, using the shared 500-episode target. For diagnosing a particular failure mechanism, seek **at least 100 distinct failing parent episodes plus matched successes pooled across relevant cells**, then split by task only where supported. That is a descriptive design target, not a powered causal test. Rare failures may require many more episodes: at the existing π0.5 spatial-500 failure rate .018, 100 failures would require about **5,556 episodes in expectation if that rate persisted**. Oversampled low-Q/failed branches can supply mechanism examples, but log their inclusion probabilities and do not use their raw frequency as deployment risk.

**Rank 7 — State-conditional policy variability, rather than one teacher draw.**

- **What / gap:** Repeated policy chunks for exactly the same observation/history, with their independent random seeds and any policy temporal state. Historical floor/resample data exist, but do not calibrate every new A/mixed anchor or another robot. The planned single shadow sample gives one teacher discrepancy, **not the intrinsic policy variation at that state**. This matters when deciding whether an action residual is unusually large.
- **Granularity / Q1 use:** Per selected anchor, aggregated per task/bank. It separates library error from stochastic teacher variation and tests whether normalized RMS is comparable between models and tasks. Multiple plausible chunks are not automatically multiple successful chunks; that requires rank 3/12 outcomes.
- **Measurement / marginal cost:** At an independently selected, pre-treatment set of anchors, obtain **three total policy draws** using isolated sampling RNGs: the planned shadow plus two extra draws. Do not advance live policy history or randomization RNG. If the policy is demonstrably deterministic for those inputs, stop redundant resampling and report that result. Extra draws really do cost inference; they are not covered by the “reuse the shadow chunk” saving.
- **Episodes/cells:** Initially **one sampled anchor in each of 200 distinct episodes per endpoint bank cell**, stratified over tasks and three Q strata; do not claim a stable task-specific 95th percentile from about 20 examples/task. Expand to 500 parent episodes where tail calibration matters. The 200-episode design adds **400 forward calls per cell**, approximately **2.2–7.3%** of the planned anchor-shadow call count if the old endpoint anchor counts apply. Those percentages are arithmetic estimates; the profiler’s actual counts must replace them.

**Rank 8 — Coverage inside a committed blind block.**

- **What / gap:** A visual/key-space state at an intermediate blind decision, linked to the originating anchor and the next observed anchor. The current proposal observes shadow policy only at vision anchors. It can therefore miss a coverage failure and recovery wholly inside a blind block. Proprioceptive telemetry alone cannot determine whether visual retrieval support broke. This is an **optional observational extension**, separate from rank 1’s factual control/state alignment.
- **Granularity / Q1 use:** Per sampled blind boundary. It answers where along execution coverage first breaks, and whether an apparently high-quality anchor masks a poor committed tail. This is a diagnostic of the quality score’s temporal resolution, not a proposed new reobservation controller.
- **Measurement / marginal cost:** Save the camera observation at selected blind boundaries without exposing it to the controller. Encode its stage-1 keys later offline, or compute an isolated shadow key and mark it as profiler-only. No policy stage-2/3 call is required; image capture/rendering, storage and one additional stage-1 encoding are real costs. Do not alter vision cadence, guards or action selection. Save the actual control index rather than assuming the blind block completed normally.
- **Episodes/cells:** Start with **one selected blind boundary in 200 distinct episodes per cell**, reusing rank 7/3 parent episodes when appropriate. This is 200 extra vision encodings, **1.1–3.7%** of the old endpoint anchor-vision counts. Use all low-level control telemetry even on unsampled episodes; expand optical sampling only if failures commonly fall between anchors. These samples are sufficient for a pooled timing/coverage screen, not narrow per-task failure-time confidence intervals.

**Rank 9 — Fresh held-out episodes, repeated run seeds, and explicit censoring/missingness.**

- **What / gap:** Outcomes and score trajectories not already inspected in this retrospective study, plus run-to-run variability at fixed bank/controller and a complete accounting of missing observations. Existing task/init IDs, accepted attempts and some repeat seeds are available; what is missing is replicated **new-profiler** validation, especially per task and library, and whether shadow/snapshot/transition failures are selectively lost. Reusing old task/init numbers with a new run is seed replication, not a new initial-condition distribution. This is partly a **collection-design requirement**.
- **Granularity / Q1 use:** Per episode/attempt/run and per-component missing-record indicator. Needed for honest calibration/test splits, uncertainty on rank/SR mappings, and avoiding “library quality” that actually predicts logging failure or censoring. Preserve reset/init-state hash, task/instruction identity, environment and policy seeds, acquisition/test membership, attempts, timeouts, crash/abort status, and shadow/snapshot completion even when an episode never reaches the ordinary success callback.
- **Measurement / marginal cost:** Preassign disjoint calibration/evaluation episode families before outcomes are inspected; freeze banks and score recipe. Keep all attempts in the audit ledger, with a declared accepted-attempt outcome rule and reasons for exclusions. Repeat matched configurations across run seeds. Scalar metadata adds negligible inference cost; repeated episodes do not. Full-pipeline LOEO refitting is offline work, not a reason to collect duplicate observations from the same trajectory.
- **Episodes/cells:** **Three run seeds on at least 100 matched task/init pairs per selected bank/controller** is a practical screening target for seed spread; expand to **500 matched pairs per seed** for decisions near the few-pp boundary. Treat original init as a cluster and do not count three repeats as three independent inits. Preserve an untouched calibration/evaluation split, for example 10%/90%, with its actual number of episodes per task reported. This can share episodes with rank 4; it is not an additional mandatory duplicate pass over every item.

**Rank 10 — Truly held-out benchmark/robot domains and their interface metadata.**

- **What / gap:** Quality/outcome calibration on a new benchmark and a different physical robot/control interface, with comparable candidate-bank regimes. π0.5 versus GR00T on the same LIBERO robot does not supply this. No logging upgrade to a LIBERO-only run can establish it. This is **outside the current experiment’s domain**.
- **Granularity / Q1 use:** Per domain/library/task/episode. This is the missing evidence for transfer of normalization and Q ranking, not merely transfer of the Python API. Record valid coordinates, physical units and frames, control rate, action scaling/saturation, camera and encoder identity, commitment duration in actual controls/time, state observability, termination/evaluation definition, task identity mapping, and bank acquisition protocol. Avoid positional conventions such as “dimension 6 is the gripper.”
- **Measurement / marginal cost:** Run the same superset schema on at least one new benchmark and one different robot; if feasible include **two held-out domain combinations** so a single fortunate transfer is not the whole evidence. Fit/calibrate the score from each new library and only its declared small recorded calibration set; reserve outcomes for validation. Real-robot execution, new libraries and sensor/interface integration are additional costs, not quantities the current simulator can synthesize.
- **Episodes/cells:** Per new domain, test **three predeclared bank regimes** (sparse/intermediate/dense or equal-count content contrasts), initially **100–200 evaluation episodes per regime** as a failure screen and **500 per regime** for confirmation of several-pp effects. With two domains that is six bank-domain units, still too few for a universal correlation claim. Add commitment-matched inference and B references if claiming gap/gain transfer, not just A ranking. Per-task precision must follow the sample-size rules above; pool hierarchically or leave task estimates unresolved when robot cost prevents sufficient sampling.

**Rank 11 — Whether profiling itself changes the policy, dynamics or apparent quality.**

- **What / gap:** Equivalence of actions/controller state with and without shadow computation, and separation of served inference cost from profiler work. The planned shadow at every anchor can advance RNG/policy state, change asynchronous timing, or perturb the rendered/physical trajectory unless isolated. Existing runtime ledgers do not validate this new observer. This is an **instrumentation-validity extension**, not a requested change to A/B.
- **Granularity / Q1 use:** Per decision and per episode, plus a profiling configuration record. Needed before interpreting a new Q–SR relation as library quality rather than observer-induced behavior. Log shadow sampling identity, actual treatment and chunk source, relevant live-state/RNG checks, control deadlines/actual timestamps, stage timings and counts, queue delays, errors, and flags distinguishing served calls, unused shadow calls, extra noise-floor draws and blind-state encodings.
- **Measurement / marginal cost:** Isolate shadow state/RNG; at selected checkpoints compare the same decision with profiling enabled/disabled or replay the baseline trace with shadow side effects suppressed. Log all timing channels without charging unused shadows to A’s *deployment* IR. Randomized serving of an already-computed shadow has zero additional forward cost relative to the profiler, exactly as planned; the physical shadow computation still belongs in the collection-cost ledger. Hash/copy checks and timing instrumentation cost CPU/I/O, and GPU-state synchronization may cost more; measure rather than assign an invented latency.
- **Episodes/cells:** Use **100 matched episode/reset tests per base cell** as a coarse semantic/observer screen, and **300 independent checks** if a zero-detected-failure result is to bound a discrepancy rate below about 1% (not prove equality). Collect timing data from all normal profiler episodes; at least 100 distinct episodes are preferable to thousands of repeated operations on one state. Full matched runs consume episodes; pure offline decision-replay checks may be cheaper but do not establish real-time physical equivalence.

**Rank 12 — The local set of successful actions, not just closeness to one policy action.**

- **What / gap:** Whether several distinct cache/library/policy actions are interchangeable for task success, and the tolerable magnitude/direction of action error around a given state. Cache and policy are only two candidate actions; shadow agreement can be poor while both succeed, or numerically excellent while a small critical error fails. Even the planned randomized experiment does not map that set. This is an **optional branch-study extension** of rank 3.
- **Granularity / Q1 use:** Per sampled anchor/action alternative with matched successors/outcomes, then per task/library. It tests a possible replacement for action-std-normalized RMS: error measured relative to empirically success-preserving action variation. It could explain why a library score ranks within settings but not across tasks or robots.
- **Measurement / marginal cost:** Preserve the candidate chunks and snapshots now. Later branch on cache, saved policy, and a small predeclared set of alternative library chunks or valid perturbations, holding continuation fixed. Respect the robot’s actuator types/constraints; do not interpolate categorical/gripper channels by a universal convention. Each extra alternative costs a branch plus any continuation inference. No new online policy inference is required merely to retain the alternatives; actual validity/success is not an offline distance calculation.
- **Episodes/cells:** Begin with **100 distinct parent episodes per selected cell and three alternatives per anchor** as a mechanism screen, then **200–500 parents** if success differences warrant calibration. Even three alternatives only probe a local part of the action set; they cannot certify a whole tolerance region. Share parents with rank 3 and count uncertainty at the parent-episode level. This is lower priority than obtaining the two fundamental cache/policy outcomes and cannot be promised from one extra logging field.

**Rank 13 — The deployment population for a “global” quality number, if it is not the benchmark’s equal-task average.**

- **What / gap:** Desired task/instruction frequencies, initial-state distribution and any operational success/cost weighting. The library’s episode proportions are acquisition choices, not necessarily deployment frequencies. A balanced simulator collection does not reveal real traffic. This is **unneeded for the report’s explicitly equal-task macro score**, but required to reinterpret it as an operational global prediction.
- **Granularity / Q1 use:** Per deployment population/library with per-episode task/init identities. It sets the averaging distribution for global Q/SR and reveals which task-level uncertainties matter most. It does not change the state-level retrieval score.
- **Measurement / marginal cost:** Declare benchmark weights as configuration, or measure task/init frequencies from representative deployment requests with sampling probabilities. No policy inference is needed just to count requests. Preserve both equal-task and population-weighted summaries; do not retroactively choose weights that improve correlation.
- **Episodes/cells:** **No new episodes** if equal-task weighting is the intended definition. If estimating one task proportion from independent requests, **385 requests** gives a worst-case approximate 5 pp half-width; simultaneous many-task or rare-task estimates need more, and repeated scripted benchmark requests are not an independent operational sample. This requirement is metadata/input to the profiler when the target population is specified externally.

**What not to ask the profiler to collect again.** The all-row LOEO scores, task counts, own-library distance scales, action normalization, candidate diversity, same-task cross-bank searches, fit/bootstrap sensitivity, and fully refitted PCA/metric LOEO are derivable from existing complete keys/actions/episode identities. The last of these was not fully computed in this report, but it is a computation gap, not missing sensor data. Existing success labels, guard outputs, fitted-library manifests and per-episode outcomes should be referenced by stable IDs; the new requirement is completeness/alignment/independence under the new experiment. Additional control policies, learned MISS budgets and triggers are not Q1’s profiler request.
