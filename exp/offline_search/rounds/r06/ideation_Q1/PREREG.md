## Estimands and choices fixed in advance

**Question:** Does an entirely library-derived risk score rank same-observation cache–policy disagreement under A, and does library quality rank A success and its shortfall from P10? These are separate questions. Disagreement with a stochastic, fallible policy is an imitation label, not action value, physical error, or probability of failure. P10 is policy at ten-control anchors; it is not a new measurement of five-control pure inference.

Primary population: accepted, client-verified A episodes, validation initializations `init % 5 != 0`, every vision anchor, including anchors near episode termination. Counterfactual *cache proposal*, including when policy is served, is compared with the independently frozen primary shadow chunk. Never use executed–shadow distance as a cache label: it is zero by construction on policy anchors. Primary horizon is the intended two decision blocks (10 here), regardless of factual truncation. First-block (5 here) and full available horizon labels are secondary. No filtering by success, achieved final progress, number of calls, final length, or future treatment.

Let action mask and block length R come from the hash-addressed library interface. Let sigma be the library's action coordinate SD over the first R controls, replacing SD <= 1e-12 by 1. Primary label E is RMS of `(cache - primary_policy)/sigma` over the first `min(2R,H)` controls and valid coordinates. Also retain RMS in logged model-normalized units. The 10/5 values are consequences of this interface, not transferred constants. Library-success selection and frozen PCA/metric fitting remain limitations of the old conditional LOEO calibration.

Four **primary**, increasing-risk candidates, fixed without pilot outcomes:

1. C: deployed task-and-regime LOEO nearest-distance midrank CDF (`retrieval.d1_loeo_quantile`).
2. D: old Q1 `d1 / cross_episode_pair_scale`, with the exact original main-metric scale (median of cross-episode distances among at most 512 evenly spaced library rows per task). Early-regime sensitivity is C; do not silently replace the old D definition.
3. R: deployed kernel-weighted mean of retrieved rows' old LOEO standardized action residual, `sum(w * err10)`.
4. Qrisk: `D + R + S`, where S is kernel-weighted LOEO two-block proprioceptive displacement residual. Missing S uses the original task mean, with observed residual weight exported. Original online quality is `1/(1+Qrisk)`. Its successor term is a predictive *feature* here, not newly validated physical dynamics.

Secondary comparators: kernel action dispersion, source V7 predicted error, and effective neighbour count (reported as negative count, so larger is less support). They cannot replace a failed primary candidate through post-hoc selection. No episode-success fitting, tuning quantiles on validation, or learned simulator predicates.

**Provenance gate:** old residual arrays must match library episode/step/task row identities and manifest hash. The old fit and P3 base must have identical retrieval feature/metric/kernel arrays and relevant settings; compare content fingerprints, not wrapper filenames. A mismatch disables the old-score claim and requires a separately versioned recalibration. C and logged labels remain analyzable. All missingness is exported; do not silently pool another task's CDF.

**Portable refit:** on A calibration initializations (`init % 5 == 0`) only, fit each candidate to E by nonnegative weighted least squares `Ehat = a + b*x`, a,b >= 0, with equal tasks, equal inits within task, equal repeats within init, equal anchors within episode. Baseline is the same weighted calibration mean E. Two coefficients per cell; no per-task pilot regression. Fit parameters, calibration episode IDs, and hashes are exported. On a new robot use the same library calibration and a few independently reserved recorded A-like trajectories; calibrate to same-state recorded policy labels, never simulator outcomes. Ten trajectories (one per task here) is a starting budget, not a precision guarantee. Production use can preserve all calibration repeats, but their dependence must not count as more scenes. Leave-one-cell-out pooled calibration is a secondary transport stress test, never evidence about an unobserved robot.

**Primary state tests:** for each candidate, compute within-episode Spearman(x,E), average repeats inside task/init, inits inside task, tasks inside cell. This avoids apparent accuracy caused only by task differences or long failures. Report per-cell and equal-cell pooled association, with paired interval for candidate minus C association. Separately compute validation episode MAE of Ehat and baseline; the primary refit gain is `(mean baseline MAE - mean candidate MAE)/mean baseline MAE` under the same hierarchy. No ratio averaging across episodes. Also compare candidate MAE with both C and D. Constant-score/constant-label episodes have undefined rho, are counted, and are excluded only from rho, not from loss. Association is conditional on episodes with rank variation.

**Exact survival rule:** after all eight complete pilot cells, a candidate survives as a portable *state-disagreement predictor* only if (i) equal-cell pooled within-episode rho >= .20 and its simultaneous lower bound > 0; (ii) validation MAE reduction >= 5% and its simultaneous lower bound > 0; (iii) point rho and loss gain are nonnegative in every complete cell; and (iv) available rho covers >= 80% of validation A episodes. Use Bonferroni two-sided confidence level `1-.05/8 = .99375` for the four candidates × two primary endpoints, 10,000 hierarchical bootstrap replicates, RNG seed 60601. Rho .20 and 5% are preregistered practical thresholds, not estimated robot constants. Per-cell/per-task/secondary intervals are descriptive 95%. Partial-cell and smoke runs never declare survival. Failure to pass is **inconclusive** unless a simultaneous upper bound is below the practical threshold, which rejects practical utility on this population. First candidate passing in order C,D,R,Qrisk is the default. A more complex candidate replaces it only with a positive simultaneous lower bound for paired MAE improvement (additional six-pair family, Bonferroni .05/6). No candidate passing means retain quality as an audit only; propose no quality gate.

**Clustering and intervals:** resample task labels, then distinct init IDs within each selected task, retaining all seed blocks and the whole vector of cohorts, library sizes and models on a shared suite/task/init. Average repeated seeds before inference. Suite task names are separate populations. Within a cell tasks have equal weight. Pooled analyses preserve matching across cells. Calibration fits are held fixed in primary validation intervals (conditional training uncertainty); the full campaign can add a separately labelled nested calibration bootstrap. Pilot validation has only one distinct init per task, so these intervals borrow task variation and describe a task-superpopulation, not conditional precision for exactly ten fixed tasks at unseen initial scenes. Per-task pilot intervals have one validation init and are explicitly unavailable. Fixed-task between-init precision is unidentified by that split. Report cluster counts and undefined estimates; no anchor-count standard errors. A cellwise fixed-task sensitivity is available only when there are >=2 validation inits per task.

**K4 secondary estimand:** on selected anchors, let U1..U4 be the primary and three resampled policy chunks. Compute unbiased coordinate sample variance V (ddof=1), mean squared cache-to-draw loss L, and signed `L - V`, which estimates squared cache-to-policy-mean error. Also retain V and cache-to-sample-mean squared error; never truncate signed excess before averaging. Use the logged selection probability via Horvitz–Thompson anchor contributions divided by the *total* eligible anchors in each episode, not selected-anchor count. Sparse selected sets, zero selections, and episode/cluster Kish ESS are reported. This is conditional head/noise variability with shared stage2, not the entire policy's epistemic uncertainty. K4 labels do not become deploy-time inputs. Same state tests on selected anchors use inverse-selection weights for descriptive association; primary survival never depends on this sparse subset.

## Global and task quality versus outcomes

Use pre-pilot `library_scores.csv` and `task_scores.csv`, LOEO and existing held-out inference/cache sources separately, with declared library mapping and provenance. Fixed increasing-risk summaries are D, reconstruction err10, and 1-Q; coverage failure is also retained. Each candidate is correlated (Spearman) with A failure rate, P10−A SR, and B−A SR. Compute per-task results (up to 80 bank/task points), eight-bank global ranks, within model/suite size contrasts, and size-adjusted residual-rank sensitivity. Resample the paired episode clusters jointly across arms/banks; the fixed library scores are not resampled. Report 95% intervals, number of distinct banks, and the four matched model/suite size pairs. With only two bank sizes, size and quality cannot generally be separated; global eight-bank ranks are descriptive, not evidence of a universal library ordering. Per-task points are dependent within bank and task. No universal SR calibration or success probability is fit with eight banks.

Also export A validation episode-mean quality versus failure as an *occupancy association*, distinguishing it from pre-existing bank quality. Whole-trajectory summaries are post-treatment occupancy descriptors, not online triggers. At-anchor Q3 input uses only the current retrieval and library calibration; Q2 input is equal-episode/task risk from reserved trajectories, including quantile-bin mass and uncertainty. Phase plots use nearest-library progress in five equal-width bins on [0,1], known at the anchor; no final episode length. First-anchor-only outcome associations are sensitivity analyses and avoid retrospective occupancy summaries.

Complete matched A/P10/B/fixed-dose cohorts have assignment probability 1, **not 1/9**. Export paired SR and deployment IR contrasts on matching task/init/seed and cohort means. P10 comparisons assess ten-control pure policy. `deployment_IR_per_request` is the primary ledger; actual-five-control IR is a truncation sensitivity. Logged profiling calls/timing are separate collection cost. No new hardware latency assumption enters quality. The pilot is not a noninferiority trial: a provisional Q2 budget may be flagged only if the simultaneous paired SR lower bound versus P10 is >= −.05 and IR is lower, with .05 a declared screening margin, not a proved acceptable owner's loss. It still needs independent validation near the intended pure-inference operating point. Minimize IR among doses satisfying that rule; failure to certify retains P10 as the reference, not an optimized budget claim.

Q3 support audit exports actual source probability, overrides, package duration/hold/delay probabilities and positive-support cluster counts. A prespecified descriptive factorial bridge uses only `override=coin`, pre-guard, 0<p_actual<1 opportunities; compare the randomized package mixture CALL versus CACHE for terminal Y within C tertiles, using HT `I(call)Y/p - I(cache)Y/(1-p)`. This is a history-weighted excursion effect of a random duration/hold package and its specified continuation, not isolated-call utility or A-controller SR. Aggregate contributions by episode (denominator all eligible opportunities), then init/task; uncertainty is clustered. Package-specific CALL(5 or 10, hold1..3) terms use `p_actual * duration_probability * hold_probability` versus CACHE `1-p_actual` (marginalize irrelevant choices on cache). Do not condition on realized delay with the marginal immediate propensity. Window and dose_mix receive support/assignment and whole-cohort outcome tables only; Q1 does not estimate their local value. No HT on deterministic hold/cap/guard/cooldown anchors; no outcome-dependent strata. These sparse package effects cannot promote a score to a deployment gate without Q3's independent value analysis.

One statistical correction supersedes the factorial paragraph's phrase “aggregate contributions by episode (denominator all eligible opportunities)”: **sum** HT contributions and eligible-opportunity counts separately within each episode, retain zero-opportunity episodes, take the prescribed task/init/seed macro means of both, then divide the two means. The resulting descriptive estimand weights histories by their opportunity frequency under the factorial controller. Duration/hold effects specify the initial duration and number of subsequent fresh-policy anchors; subsequent durations and all remaining actions follow the logged controller. ### Remaining data limitations and falsification

The new direct labels resolve the old missing same-observation teacher comparison. K4 distinguishes some head-sampling spread from systematic cache disagreement. Neither supplies the policy's physical correctness, the full model's uncertainty under different upstream encodings, an A-versus-policy counterfactual successor on the identical simulator state, a certified snapshot restore, independent alternative libraries, or another robot. The ranked wishlist in `REPORT.md`/`DATA_WISHLIST.md` still applies to those missing quantities.

A primary score with negligible rank signal or no held-out loss gain is rejected or left inconclusive by the fixed rule; a reconstruction score that adds nothing to C/D is not selected. Strong state prediction with weak/discordant A-outcome ranks supports an imitation diagnostic only. An apparent old Q advantage disappearing after matching size, changing occupancy, or leaving a cell out falsifies the corresponding transfer claim. If K4 variance explains the large distances, “wrong cache” must be weakened to disagreement with a noisy teacher. If new independently acquired banks or a new robot reverse the ordering, the global quality claim fails even if the pilot state tests pass. There is no scientific survivor to report before the pilot analysis is run.
