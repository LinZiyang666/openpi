# R8 E2 — Where a call or a look is worth it

## 1. Summary

**Recommendation:** collect same-observation alternatives at every five-control decision, including blind decisions; estimate local call value from audited randomization; keep snapshot branches behind an explicit restoration certificate. A stage can predict disagreement, failure, or intervention benefit; these are three separate validation targets. Do not build another allocation rule during this collection round.

Read: the complete [R8 brief](../../IDEATION_BRIEF.md); R6 [SCHEMA_V2](../../../r06/p3_profiling/SCHEMA_V2.md), relevant handback/branch sections, `design.py`, `telemetry.py`, `snapshots.py`, and `v2_engine.py`; ordinary plugin logging and C/CU/CT assignment code; R7 stage API, C4 tools, E2 proposal/profile analysis, E1 profile analysis, and ANALYSIS including wrist-price and replicate addenda; the original trace quality report and trace record schema. Checked real files with the bounded audit in §5.

Three findings determine the proposal:

- The 16 CU/CT non-test profiles contain **320 accepted episodes**. Sparse CT has **0/81 supported deviation entries**; dense CT has **35/85**. A deterministic entry call and a cooldown exclusion do not form a randomized experiment. CU preserves substantially more local support.
- P3 already records much of the desired evidence, including blind fresh-cache proposals and both alternative wire chunks at anchors. Its client rejects ordinary servers, and its private policy sample can become the executed sample. Reusing it as generic debug mode requires explicit behavior-invariance work.
- The two inspected snapshot episodes have real contacts/poses, but `grasp_slip_release` is literally **unannotated**, and every snapshot remains uncertified. Four sampled pure-inference HDF5 headers have no simulator-state/contact/object-pose field names. Historical L5 traces are valuable diagnostics, not substitutes for new L10 physical trajectories.

All work here is specification and bounded CPU analysis. No simulator, policy inference, server, worker, chain, remote operation, GPU, or git operation was run. Writes are confined to this directory and `/tmp/r8_E2_call_value/`.

### 2.1 Action comparisons and stages

Use manifest action dimensions, coordinate conventions, valid horizons and library-fitted scales. Current LIBERO comparisons use seven valid channels; **never use the 25 GR00T padding channels**. Report six motion channels and gripper separately, including wire-command transition timing and measured aperture. A sign difference alone is not an annotated failed grasp.

At observation time `t`, save `tail_t`, `fresh_cache_t`, `policy_t`, and the executed proposal. Compare their first five future controls from the **same t**; additionally report a ten-control proposal comparison only where all alternatives support it. Align a carried chunk using its original control cursor. A policy chunk from the previous anchor is not a fresh policy comparison at a blind state. Physical outcome metrics use actual execution masks; unexecuted proposal horizons remain explicitly diagnostic.

Record stage **before** the treatment coin using only the available history, plus its detector/version/confidence. Keep the triggering parent stage, newly observed stage, and simulator-derived retrospective stage as distinct columns. A fresh observation can change the label: using that post-LOOK label as the LOOK moderator leaks the treatment. The same issue applies to progress computed from the selected policy action, future success, terminal episode fraction, or post-hoc alignment to another trajectory.

For K=4 independent policy draws at an independently sampled 1/32 of decisions, report policy–policy versus cache–policy squared error. Subtracting half the expected squared distance between two independent policy draws estimates the noise contribution to cache–policy squared error; it does **not** make the mean policy action optimal. Keep negative finite-sample adjusted estimates and uncertainty, and keep multimodal gripper statistics separate. Sampling three extra heads at 1/32 adds 9.375% head dispatches to an all-shadow pass; measure their real cost.

### 2.2 What randomized calls identify

Let `H_t` be pre-assignment history, `Z_t` the randomized CALL indicator, and `p_t=P(Z_t=1|H_t)` **after** all availability/guard/cooldown overrides. At fresh supported anchors, use:

`w_t = Z_t/p_t - (1-Z_t)/(1-p_t)`.

For the first supported entry `τ_s` into a frozen stage, average `I(τ_s reached) × w_τ × [Y - b(H_τ)]` over the original episode population. `b` is an optional cross-fitted baseline. Unreached episodes contribute zero; report their share. This estimates a one-time CALL-versus-CACHE excursion after the behavior-policy prefix, under the original future controller. Also report the conditional reached-entry contrast with its explicitly different denominator. Selection of `τ_s` must be a pre-treatment stopping rule.

Summing supported anchor scores within an episode estimates the derivative of success under a small additive shift to that stage's call probability, with the original continuation and interior support. It is **not** an independent-anchor success rate or the value of replacing the entire controller. Never divide an episode's score by its eventual stage duration. A proposed finite controller change needs its own closed-loop comparison; products of long-horizon importance weights are not the P0 answer.

CU is the main natural experiment. Reconstruct its exact rule: cooldown `p=0`, confirmed stall otherwise `p=1`, ordinary/ambiguous states `p=nominal`; ambiguous CACHE can schedule an early LOOK. Consequently its local treatment includes that downstream continuation, not merely exchanging two otherwise identical ten-control chunks. CT's effective interior probability is usable; its saturated entries are not. C4 currently accepts the R6 `os_q2_*` dose schema, so merely running it on CU logs is insufficient.

For two-level R6 dose arms, keep the conditional anchor `p_d`; use the separate dose factor `g(d)/π(d)` only when changing the dose target. Do not substitute `π(d)p_d` for the CALL probability and `1-π(d)p_d` for CACHE. Deterministic holds/delays/caps have no local support; their upstream package assignment is the usable randomization.

Primary outcomes: accepted final success, plus predeclared goal-predicate/contact/relative-pose changes within 5/10/20 **active controls**, remaining work, remaining controls, and time to task termination. Treat success/failure terminals as absorbing for a fixed-horizon outcome; infrastructure loss is missing, never ordinary failure. A physical failure detector's output remains a versioned label with uncertainty. Cost and event outcomes must use the same supported population as SR.

Report `p` distribution, forced shares, observed branches, both weighted ESSs, unique task/init clusters, and unsupported stages. Bootstrap task/init clusters within fixed tasks, keeping all arms/seeds and snapshots for that pair together; distinguish fixed-benchmark uncertainty from generalization to new tasks. If a nuisance model is refit-dependent, refit it inside bootstrap or explicitly label conditional uncertainty. Use direct stage-by-treatment interaction intervals, not “significant here, nonsignificant there.” Preserve the family of all tested stage definitions and held-task checks.

### 2.3 Error growth and the value of looking

Use controls since the last **consumed encoder result**, separately per camera and since last full look, cache refresh and policy call. Physical camera capture, a diagnostic encoder run, and a controller-consumed look are different events. The first blind A request is age five controls even when the plugin's pre-request blind counter is zero.

Plot `tail–policy`, `fresh-cache–policy`, and their paired difference versus age, with current state-to-demo successor residual, full/wrist disagreement and physical object/EE residual beside them. Normalize proprioceptive residuals with frozen successful-library scales. Report cohort sizes at each age, within-parent-anchor changes and action provenance (native tail versus successor stitch). Do not splice different episodes into one apparent error-growth trajectory.

An age-ten survivor has already avoided earlier LOOK/termination. Therefore ordinary age curves and early-look hazard are descriptive; a falling error curve can reflect selection. Record the support/valve/stage/call/terminal reason each path leaves the risk set. IP weighting only helps when the observation/extension probability is known and positive; deterministic gates cannot identify excluded tails. Group-4 randomized or whole-controller contrasts supply intervention evidence beyond these curves.

At a blind state, fresh retrieval closer to a shadow policy is an **opportunity proxy**, not observed rescue. Looking and replacing the remaining action are separate interventions. A diagnostic LOOK whose output is never consumed cannot improve robot behavior by construction; adding a separate 500-episode arm for that no-op is wasteful. An information-only treatment needs a specified downstream decision rule that actually uses the early information.

### 2.4 Snapshot branches: feasible in principle, not certified today

P3 v2 is sufficient to preserve candidate branch points, but not to claim counterfactual outcomes. Its client snapshot is pre-inference while recorded method diagnostics can be post-query/pre-action; restoration must not apply the state update twice. The v1 warning about absent alternate wire actions is superseded by v2 `candidate_wire` at anchors. Blind alternate wire chunks still need an explicit generic contract.

Certification requirements for the later executor:

1. Freeze scene/task/init/XML/assets, simulator/controller/renderer/policy/plugin versions, fits, action transforms and remaining task deadline. Export dynamic physics, actuator/solver state exposed by that version, environment/wrapper counters, goal state, controller goals/filters/interpolators, observation caches, and every RNG. Classify each skipped field as immutable/reconstructible/stateful with evidence; a `None` interpolator alone is not a missing dynamic state.
2. Export or deterministically reconstruct server history: real vision masks and keys, observation/action/hit buffers, anchor and gripper memo, guard/stall windows, stage latch, pending policy/cache tail cursor, planned LOOK, budget/delay/hold/cooldown state, and serving RNG streams. Diagnostic blind keys must never become factual history. Hash the exact pre-query boundary.
3. First restore into a fresh isolated environment and replay **saved factual wire controls**, checking each successor state, observation, contacts/predicates, termination and deadline. Check 5/10/20 controls and the remaining factual suffix. This certifies physical replay only.
4. Then resume the **original live controller** from the restored checkpoint and compare its next keys, retrieval, actions, guard verdicts, RNG positions and eventual result with the factual run. This separately certifies policy/controller continuation; physics replay alone does not.
5. Scope the certificate by software/hardware/batching manifest and state strata. Use 100 distinct parent episodes across tasks/models/suites and contact/free-motion/boundary/late states, then 300 if clean. Zero mismatches in 300 independent parents bounds pooled mismatch probability at approximately 1% (one-sided 95%), not per-cell or universal exactness. Multiple snapshots of one parent do not multiply this denominator.
6. Require bitwise equality where deterministic; otherwise preregister field-level tolerances from independent factual-repeat checks and label the certificate numerical, not exact. Changed treatment assignment, guard verdict, contact/goal outcome or termination fails even if position errors are small. Publish every failure, not just accepted easy snapshots; acceptance coverage is part of the target population.
7. Only then fork identical states into fixed-duration cache versus policy, or a named delay package, with common future RNG streams and the same remaining deadline. Record each branch's actual work and termination. Aggregate by parent task/init; never regard siblings as independent episodes. A certificate scoped to one adapter is not transferable to another robot.

If certification fails, retain snapshots and use randomized live assignments for causal estimates. Do not relabel approximate teleports as exact counterfactuals. No restoration, branch execution or certification is attempted by this explorer.

### 2.5 Per-stage accounting

For full-camera decisions, deployment work is `K = c_v V + c_m M`, with `(c_v,c_m)=(.152,.848)` for π0.5 and `(.148,.852)` for GR00T. Report owner `K/N_decisions` and separately `5K/N_actual_active_controls`; exclude settling controls. Keep sum-of-work, actual controls and termination alongside every ratio. A full L10 policy is approximately .5 because it calls once per two decisions; a fresh-anchor call adds only the action-head price when the look is already paid.

Attribute each invocation once to its pre-treatment stage; report a separate trigger-stage view for an early LOOK charged to the parent decision. Unknown stages retain their own bucket. Stage work must sum exactly to arm work. For within-stage intensity use actual control exposure; for share of total arm IR use the arm denominator. Also publish equal-episode means and paired differences: they are not the pooled ratio of totals. Present joint benefit/work intervals; a ratio of estimated benefit to nearly zero incremental work is not a stable stage ranking. A beneficial call can reduce later calls or shorten an episode, so its total work effect need not equal the immediate head price.

For wrist-only paths retain camera dispatch/completion counts and versioned prices. R7's assumed `.055198` wrist price differs from its measured `.0646`; report owner-assumed and measured-hardware ledgers separately, including measured full-path overhead. All shadow forwards, extra draws, diagnostic retrieval, encoding and I/O have a separate **collection** ledger. All-shadow wall latency cannot establish uninstrumented serving latency.

**Look-package probe specification:** run A before a single probe; at each structurally valid blind decision five controls after a cache anchor, select it with independent hazard 1/16 until the first selection. Before seeing any branch result, assign `{continue saved tail, fresh full-look cache replacement, full-look policy replacement}` with probabilities `{1/3,1/3,1/3}`. Each serves the next five controls, then resumes the same A rule at the common next scheduled anchor with a fresh full LOOK. Invalidate unused tails at that boundary while preserving elapsed time and each branch's actual action/observation history; never treat the resumed state as episode step zero. Log these continuation semantics, including five-control policy commitment in the probe. Stop normally if terminal. No further probes. Retain unselected episodes and the selection hazard/reach rate. This isolates a five-control **look-and-act package**, not information alone.

**Timing-probe specification:** A baseline, independent hazard 1/16 at fresh anchors, cap one trigger per episode. At the first trigger assign `{CALL now, CALL at the next A anchor ten controls later}` with probability 1/2 each, hold=1 and ten-control policy commitment; A everywhere else and afterward. Record due/control clocks, availability, assignment probability and realized cost. An episode ending before the delayed call stays in its assigned group. This is an intention-to-treat placement package, **not** a comparison conditional on both arms spending a call, and not guaranteed equal realized IR. P3 delay machinery is a reuse candidate after generic integration, not something to launch unchanged.

In a 500-episode three-way probe, even full reach gives only about 167 episodes per choice; a worst-case binary-outcome difference has a roughly ±10.7 pp 95% margin before stage subdivision. Thus its P1 deliverable is mechanism/proximal evidence and coarse effect bounds, not a finely ranked stage policy. More anchors do not create more independent final outcomes.

**Execution/storage envelope:** 56×500×60 MB = **1.68 TB**, before duplicate archives/derived tables. A 20% working allowance gives 2.016 TB, leaving only ~84 GB of the stated 2.1 TB free. Target **≤50 MB mean/episode** (1.4 TB raw; ~1.68 TB with allowance), preferably 35–45 MB using deduplication/binary controls. At 100 decisions, raw full keys alone add ~26 MB; images add 30–39 MB before lossless compression, so the full-key tier is the first optional volume knob. Do not generate full duplicated CSV exports or keep receiver tarballs indefinitely. Archive decisions belong to the coordinator; no old data is moved here.

The R7 rate implies an unprofiled 5.6-hour arithmetic floor, not a debug forecast. Full shadows at every decision make A GPU work closer to L5 inference than L10 serving. Planning scenarios at debug throughputs of 2.5k/1k/0.5k episodes/hour are 11.2/28/56 hours, before retries/finalization; these rates are hypothetical. Require the integration smoke to report actual per-cell throughput, bytes/episode p50/p95, forward counts and queue pressure, then replace these scenarios. If constrained, first defer the two P2 timing arms, then the two look-package arms; retain the 28 P0 arms and the systematic lever comparisons before increasing snapshot or noise-draw density.

## 5. Preliminary evidence and reproduction

## 6. Risks, open decisions, and completion criteria for the tools

- **Segmentation leakage:** R8 eval500 can discover hypotheses, but cannot independently confirm stage definitions chosen using its outcomes. Freeze current labels on library/non-test data; mark new post-hoc segmentations exploratory and require later untouched confirmation. Keep simulator-only labels diagnostic so the same tool can accept robot poses/tactile data with explicit missing fields.
- **Support versus power:** CU gives natural randomized excursions only where not forced. Do not infer stalled-state rescue value from forced calls or extrapolate dense CT support to sparse CT. Report interval width and actual call/cache episode counts; null results are not proof of safe call suppression.
- **Continuation dependence:** a useful call under A or the isolated package can be harmful under CU's stall/LOOK history. Report controller/commitment identity with every value estimate; cross-continuation sign checks are required before a stage allocation claim.
- **Shared initial states:** pure/cache paired trajectories permit outcome comparisons and divergence timelines, but after action paths separate, equal control index or DTW alignment is not the same causal state. Pair restoration is stronger only after certification.
- **No cheap oracle:** policy samples can fail; gripper-mode disagreement can be benign; simulator contacts do not define intent. Demand physical proximal evidence and randomized benefit before naming a stage “call-worthy.”
- **Debug coupling:** complete RNG isolation, live/history masks, scheduling and precision must be checked by integration. The current P3 experimental wrapper is not itself proof of action equality for an arbitrary method.
- **Resource uncertainty:** compression, contacts and server histories can dominate size; all-shadow GPU overhead is not represented by owner IR. Keep byte/forward ledgers and declared coverage, and resolve sampling tiers using non-test collection measurements rather than outcomes.
- **P0 acceptance:** all 500 accepted episodes/arm join exactly; no silent treatment-field truncation; actual controls/chunks and actual conditional propensities reconcile; every stage sum equals arm work; shadow missingness/provenance is explicit; blind-age and terminal masks are correct; unsupported contrasts are withheld. A tool should produce the same report schema for either model and null-with-reason for unavailable benchmark sensors.
- **Coordinator decisions still needed:** final E4 use of its 20-arm reserve, exact valve timing for the nested gate ablation, measured full-key/storage tier, inline versus deferred shadows, and whether the two P2 timing arms fit the schedule. These are concrete selection points, not requests to launch anything during ideation.
