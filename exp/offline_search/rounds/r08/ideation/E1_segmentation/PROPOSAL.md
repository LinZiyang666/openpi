# R8 E1 — Measure interaction stages, then test risk and call value separately

## 1. Summary and scope

**Recommendation: collect a method-independent, control-resolution physical timeline plus decision-resolution cache/policy alternatives; compare several segmenters offline on the same trajectories. Do not select a new serving segmentation before this collection.** A stage boundary, a cache-risk score, and the benefit of calling the policy are three different quantities. R8 should measure each and test their relationship.

Read the R8 brief and R6 `SCHEMA_V2.md` in full. Examined R7 E1 `PROPOSAL.md` and `PROFILE_ANALYSIS.md`, `stages/STAGES_API.md`, the C4/profile READMEs, relevant R7 analysis/selection, C3 call semantics, R6 telemetry/reader/hand-back/streaming material, closed-loop README, trace writer/schema and trace-quality reports. Primary research sources are linked below. The brief's R7 E1 path resolves to `r07/ideation/E1_kinematic_stages/`.

New read-only checks: eight library banks, three step schemas in each of eight old trace files, and four accepted R6 A episodes (task 0/init 0, one per model/suite). [evidence.py](evidence.py) ran in 5.09 s on the assigned CPUs, without serving imports or simulator/model execution. Results are in `/tmp/r8_E1_segmentation/evidence.json`; §5 gives the command and denominators.

Three findings change the measurement priorities:

- **67.4–79.3% of low-speed library rows are outside R7's ±1-row gripper-event neighborhoods.** This measures omitted kinematic structure, not failure risk; normal settling is also slow.
- All **1,199/1,199** active controls in the four sampled R6 episodes have some contact. Table/object support contacts make an undifferentiated contact bit useless here. We need entity-resolved contact topology and relative motion.
- R6 already contains physical measurements and task predicates, but its `grasp_slip_release` field explicitly says **unannotated**. In the sampled traces, consecutive `before` states equal the preceding `after` state in **1,195/1,195** pairs. Deduplicating states and entity catalogs is preferable to sacrificing temporal resolution.

No new rollout, server, worker, chain, simulator, GPU use, remote-host operation, or Git operation was performed. This is a tooling/experiment specification; the only executable deliverable is a small analysis script.

## 2. Segmentation questions, tools, and validation

### Representation and candidate families

Represent a trajectory with **nested boundaries and uncertainty**, rather than a mandatory universal approach/grasp/place script. A macro interaction may contain transit, alignment, acquisition, transport, placement or release; repeated interactions, regrasp, abort/retry and unknown states must remain possible. These semantic names are annotations requiring evidence, not synonyms for gripper polarity or low speed. Success in Spatial need not await release.

Every candidate exports `episode, control_start, control_end_exclusive, parent_segment, boundary_type, confidence, support, feature_tier, fit_hash`. A second product exports the **causal** decision-time posterior over stages, entropy, unknown mass, latest observation time and delay. Retrospective intervals and causal estimates must be separate columns. Preserve anonymous IDs when semantics are uncertain; stage IDs from independent fits need explicit matching before cross-model comparison.

Portable input tier: timestamps, issued commands, proprioception, calibrated end-effector pose/finger measurements, and cameras already available to the robot. Optional tier: force/tactile sensing or estimated object frames with uncertainty. Simulator object state, contacts and predicates are **privileged evaluation/teacher labels**; they must never enter retrieval, call allocation, or a purported portable online tracker. Task identity may select its demonstration bank, as A already does; do not add task-name rules.

Do not collapse these signals into an equal-weight “difficulty” sum. Start with separate features and ablations. Use a low-complexity event/stop hierarchy as the primary descriptive baseline; geometry and learned sequences must earn additional complexity.

**Initial benchmark configuration, frozen on discovery data:** retain exact R7 as G0; G1 adds low-speed runs at training speed quantiles {10%, 25%}, with dwell {1, 5, 10} controls; G2 adds geometric knots at {0.5, 1, 2} times the median training one-control displacement, scaling translation and angular increments separately. Retain all sensitivity curves. For PELT compare standardized feature mean-shift costs with penalties {1, 2, 4}×feature-dimension×log(sequence length), minimum segment length five controls, and an event-constrained variant. The HSMM screen uses K={4, 8, 16}, at most 16 frozen feature dimensions and learned durations; select by held-demo likelihood/boundary quality, never R8 holdout outcomes. These are screening grids, not claimed universal physical thresholds. Do not require matching final transition counts to align demos; that would exclude retries and use future information in an online tracker.

### Validation contract: what would convince us

1. **Data and causality gates.** Require complete accepted-attempt/control joins, no reset crossing, exact issued-action/chunk-offset correspondence, explicit partial terminal commitments and field missingness. Initial state precedes control 0; include a final post-control state. At decision d, expose only observations actually available before its choice. Perturb/delete future suffixes and verify causal stage outputs through d do not change. Smoothed labels remain descriptive only.

2. **Independent physical labels.** E3 owns failure taxonomy; E1 needs onset intervals for missed acquisition, loss of transport, wrong-object interaction, misplacement and persistent alignment/stall. Failed terminal episodes alone are not onset labels. Contact plus aperture plus object/EE co-motion can support acquisition; commanded opening, support contact and placement predicates help distinguish intended release from slip. Ambiguous intent stays unknown. Store label rule/version, confidence and reviewer evidence.

3. **Annotation audit.** Before calling automatic events ground truth, double-label 200 short windows: 100 uniformly sampled and 100 sampled around candidate boundaries, stratified across model/suite and success/failure. Log inclusion probabilities. Report inter-rater agreement and weighted precision/recall; adjudicate disagreements. Use a separate held-out annotation subset for event-detector validation. Annotate onset uncertainty intervals: five-control imagery alone cannot certify one-control visual timing. Compare boundary F1 at ±1, ±5 and ±10 actual controls where label resolution supports it, duration error and over-segmentation; matched boundary count prevents a boundary-at-every-tick “win.”

4. **Independent splits.** Fit unsupervised geometry/scales on successful library demos; fit risk calibration and any outcome-supervised segmenter only on designated discovery data. Group all arms, models, seeds and retries of a task/init (plus reset-hash aliases) in one fold. Check bank/evaluation init overlap; overlapping states limit held-init claims even if outcome labels are excluded. R6's two test inits/task and prior R7 results are already inspected. They are discovery evidence, not a new confirmation set. Reserve within-task positions 30–49 of the authoritative R8 init manifest as a locked analysis holdout before outcome inspection; use positions 0–29 for discovery with nested folds, keeping all 500 for frozen arm-level comparisons. Do not assume original init IDs are contiguous. This creates 200 paired holdout clusters/cell before alias/overlap exclusions, not 500 fresh clusters; prior inspection of these same states still limits confirmatory claims. If the coordinator uses all 500 for exploration, label results exploratory and seek later confirmation.

5. **Boundary quality is necessary but insufficient.** Compare gripper-only, elapsed-control/time baselines using training lengths, equal-count uniform knots, event+stop, and each refinement. Final episode length/progress, number of future transitions, smoothed alignment and eventual failure must not be online features. Match hard-stage exposure at 10/20/30% using discovery thresholds; report realized exposure and unknown coverage on holdout instead of recomputing holdout quantiles.

6. **Risk endpoint.** Primary target: independently labelled bad physical-event onset within the next **20 actual controls** of an at-risk cache decision; 5/10-control windows are secondary. Stop first-event risk exposure at the first onset; analyze recurrent events separately. Successful completion is an absorbing competing endpoint, not a dropped/censored success. Time limits without an observed onset remain right-censored, with truncated counts reported. Report pure-A risk separately from mixed-policy exposure. Summarize precision–recall/AUPRC relative to prevalence, Brier calibration, sensitivity at 20% flagged exposure and median actionable lead. Within-stage policy disagreement is secondary. Cluster uncertainty by task/init across all replicates, report each cell and equal-task aggregates; nearby decisions are not independent trials.

7. **Suggested retention rule, fixed before holdout.** At the frozen ~20% exposure threshold, require improvement in onset sensitivity over R7 with a positive pooled paired 95% CI and no model/suite average point reversal; report all eight cells and adjust the primary candidate comparisons for multiplicity (e.g. Holm). Require median detection lead of at least one five-control decision for an anticipatory routing claim. If few onset episodes or wide CIs prevent this conclusion, keep descriptive labels without declaring a hard/easy classifier. An onset-time detector may still be useful for diagnosis.

8. **Call-value endpoint.** On supported randomized fresh anchors, estimate a stage-conditioned local excursion effect on final success and short-horizon physical progress under the recorded continuation. Stage assignment must precede the coin and cannot depend on the chosen action; cache proposal features may be computed before assignment. Use actual conditional probabilities after stall/cap/cooldown rules, and cluster repeated encounters. Report at least CALL/CACHE counts, ESS and contributing init clusters; treat fewer than 30 distinct supported init clusters per stage as exploratory. Neither high cache risk nor low cache–policy error proves positive call value. Local effects cannot certify a whole equal-cost stage controller; a later frozen prospective comparison must do that.

9. **Transfer.** Show both leave-task-out evaluation (any access to held-task unlabeled demos stated explicitly) and frozen-rule model/suite transfer with only adapter/library scale changes. Report performance for real portable inputs versus privileged poses/contacts. A successful simulator oracle with a failed portable tracker is a representation gap, not a deployable segmentation. R8 should expose this gap rather than hide it behind one pooled AUROC.

## 6. Risks and open questions for the coordinator/coders

- **Semantic identifiability:** a commanded grasp with no object, a legitimate contact pause, and a failed alignment can share kinematics. Preserve multiple possible labels and review images; do not force a grasp-success label from closure.
- **Contact sampling:** end-of-control contacts can miss short collisions. Compact substep pair/impulse aggregates are useful, but must be measured without changing integration; solver-array snapshots alone do not resolve event order.
- **Library GT gap:** old banks lack physical object/contact trajectories. Relative bottleneck fitting initially uses new pure-policy/discovery trajectories; those remain separate diagnostic demonstrations, not silent replacements for A's frozen banks. Confirm whether source library observations can ever be joined to richer physical data before promising retrospective object-frame fits.
- **Causal tracker versus offline atlas:** future-smoothed gripper modes, PELT, DTW and waypoint extraction can make offline boundaries look excellent. Their online predictive value must be measured with prefixes and actual camera availability. New labels do not justify extra blind execution by themselves.
- **Cross-model action units and multimodality:** decoded rotations and gripper polarity need authoritative adapters. Two valid policy modes can disagree strongly. The resample noise floor and physical endpoints prevent an action RMS threshold from becoming a fictional safety boundary.
- **Randomization support and power:** CU's forced calls and cooldowns remove local support; dense Spatial has few failures. Probe arms improve support but 500 episodes still may not resolve rare stage-specific effects. Do not turn wide intervals into “no benefit,” or state-change correlations into causal value.
- **Implementation boundaries:** reuse P3 telemetry/isolation/strict readers but remove the v2-method-only client dependency. E5 owns generic integration and invariance; E3 owns physical labels; E2 owns causal estimands/restoration; E4 owns final lever scheduling. Tool interfaces above should survive their refinements.
- **Outstanding choices:** finalize probe versus VF1 slots; attest bank/reset overlap and lock the holdout manifest; validate entity/target-frame adapters for articulated tasks; measure per-model all-shadow throughput and storage before committing the full campaign. These are coordinator decisions, not permission requests or actions taken by E1.
